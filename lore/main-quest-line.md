# Main Quest Line: Rise of The Docks

## The Path to Dr. Victor "The Chemist" Crane

The final confrontation awaits after you've built your empire. But first, you must prove yourself worthy of facing the most dangerous man in The Docks.

## ACT I: THE TUTORIAL (Quests 1-5)

### Quest 1: First Steps
**Type:** Tutorial  
**Location:** Starting Apartment  
**Difficulty:** Trivial  

**Objective:** Meet your联络人 (contact) at the corner store. Learn the basics of the neighborhood and receive your first package.

**Objectives:**
- Walk to the corner store on 5th & Main
- Speak with "Shorty" the store owner
- Receive your first delivery package
- Deliver it to the apartment complex across the street

**Rewards:**
- +50 XP
- Starter cash: $100
- Unlock: Basic drug processing UI

### Quest 33: The Final Confrontation
**Type:** Boss Battle  
**Location:** Crane's Laboratory  
**Difficulty:** EXTREME  

**Objective:** Face Dr. Victor Crane. End the meth nightmare.

**Boss: Dr. Victor "The Chemist" Crane**
- **Appearance:** Wild, bloodshot eyes that dart uncontrollably, jittery movements, stained lab coat covered in chemical burns, meth cook aesthetic with beakers and tubes everywhere
- **Personality:** Paranoid, manic energy, sees himself as a revolutionary chemist, speaks in rapid-fire monologues about "the formula"
- **Health:** 500
- **Attacks:**
  - Chemical Spray (AoE damage)
  - Explosive Flasks (projectile)
  - Mind Warp (confusion)
  - Meth Fury (enraged state, increased damage)
  - Lab Explosion (environment hazard)

**Objectives:**
- Defeat Dr. Crane
- Destroy his meth operation
- Claim his research (or destroy it)
- Escape the exploding lab

**Rewards:**
- +5000 XP
- Crane's Recipe Book (all recipes)
- Legendary status
- **Victory!**
