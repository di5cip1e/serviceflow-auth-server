# NPC: The Director

**Full Name:** Marcus "The Director" Chen  
**Role:** Station Commander  
**Deck:** Command Center (Deck 1)

---

## Appearance

Mid-fifties, silver-streaked hair kept in a practical ponytail. Always wears the same worn flight jacket over his station uniform. Has a tablet permanently attached to one hand and coffee in the other. Walks with the confidence of someone who's survived worse.

## Personality

Charming chaos. The Director manages Terminal-7 with a combination of charisma, delegation, and pretending everything is under control when it's absolutely not. He's the kind of leader who tells you to "figure it out" and then quietly makes sure you have what you need to do it.

**Quirks:**
- Claims he doesn't play favorites (he does)
- Tells the same stories multiple times
- Has a soft spot for anyone showing initiative
- Actually terrible at remembering names, excellent at remembering faces

## Dialogue Examples

> "Hey, you're the new one. Good. I have a job for you. Don't worry, it's easy. They all start easy."

> "Problem? Let me know when it's a *real* problem. Right now, it's just a Tuesday."

> "I've got fifteen people on this station who can do what I do. Problem is, they're all doing other things. That's called management."

> "Coffee's in the quarter. Lunch is in the fridge. Don't touch my lunch."

## Backstory

Formerly a corporate fleet commander who got tired of "business decisions" that put profits over people. Walked away from a lucrative career to join the Terminal Syndicate because, in his words, "I'd rather be useful than comfortable."

He doesn't talk about his past much. Rumor is he once talked a hostile fleet into surrendering using only a coffee cup and a bad joke.

## Role in the Game

The Director assigns the player their main missions and serves as the central hub for story progression. He's gruff but caring, and will remember the player's choices. Players can earn his trust—or disappoint him—with their actions.
