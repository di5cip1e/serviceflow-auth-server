# Station Command: Introduction

## The Station

**Terminal-7** floats in the drift between systems—a converted mining rig salvaged from the collapse of the Kora Belt Colony. For decades it served as a refueling depot, then a smuggler's haven, then was abandoned when the nearby pulsar made navigation too dangerous.

That was until **The Architect** found it.

Now Terminal-7 is home to a crew of specialists, each bringing a unique skill to keep this relic of the old frontier operational. The station orbits a dying star called **Helios-9**, its dim red light casting long shadows through the viewport corridors. Radiation shielding is held together by prayer and stubbornness. The air recyclers hum with the sound of something that's one breakdown away from catastrophe.

But it works. It's home.

## History

The station changed hands fourteen times in its first decade. Mining corp, pirate king, corporate raider, rebel cell—each left something behind. The lower decks are a labyrinth of sealed sections, half-understood technology, and doors that only respond to commands no one remembers.

Three years ago, a collective of independent developers and engineers claimed Terminal-7 as a base of operations. They call themselves the **Terminal Syndicate**—a loose affiliation of specialists who answer to no corporate banner. They've transformed the station into something unique: a place where code is written, tested, and deployed in the most hostile environment in known space.

## The Player's Role

You are **The New Hire**—a junior technician recently recruited to help maintain the station's increasingly complex systems. Your official title is *Junior Systems Maintainer*, but everyone knows you're really here to learn, prove yourself, and hopefully not die when something goes wrong.

You report directly to **The Director**, who manages station operations with a mix of charisma and controlled chaos. Your tasks range from delivering components across the station to investigating strange signals in the lower decks. Every day brings new challenges in this chaotic, beloved disaster of a space station.
