# Mission Briefs

## Mission 1: The Mystery of the Missing Lunch

**Type:** Investigation / Comedy  
**Location:** Deck 2 - Living Quarters  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Briefing
The Director's lunch has vanished from the galley refrigerator. This wouldn't be a problem except for three things: it was *the good lunch* (real coffee and everything), the Director is in a mood, and someone left a sarcastic note in its place.

*"Thanks for the meal. Could use more mustard next time."*

### Objectives
- Interview potential witnesses in the galley
- Search the Rec Room for suspicious activity
- Identify the culprit (likely a crew member pulling a prank)
- Recover or replace the lunch before The Director notices

### Rewards
- Access to the Director's private snack stash (permanent)
- +10 reputation with The Director

---

## Mission 2: Signal in the Static

**Type:** Surveillance / Exploration  
**Location:** Deck 5 - Lower Decks  
**Difficulty:** Medium

### Briefing
The monitoring station picked up an unusual transmission from an abandoned section of the lower decks. It's not an emergency signal, but it's definitely *something*—a repeating pattern that doesn't match any known station system.

A.R.I.A. is curious. That's enough to be concerned.

### Objectives
- Acquire access card from Engineering
- Navigate the lower deck corridors (watch for hazards)
- Locate the source of the transmission
- Record what you find without touching anything obviously dangerous
- Report back to The Director

### Rewards
- Access to previously locked lower deck sections
- Unlocks side quest: "The Ghost in the Machine"
- +15 reputation with A.R.I.A.

---

## Mission 3: The Forge Has Gone Weird

**Type:** Crisis / Repair  
**Location:** Deck 3 - Engineering  
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

### Briefing
The Forge (the 3D fabricator) has started producing objects that weren't in its design library. It printed a small brass key that fits no known lock. Then a component that doesn't match any system on the station. Now it's humming in a way that makes the lights flicker.

The Engine Mechanic thinks it's possessed. The Blueprint Architect thinks it's evolving. The QA Interrogator thinks someone is hacking it.

You need to figure out which one it is—and stop it before it prints something dangerous.

### Objectives
- Interview the Engine Mechanic and Blueprint Architect
- Check the Forge's error logs
- Decide: bug, ghost, or hack?
- Fix the problem (method depends on your conclusion)
- Document findings for the records

### Rewards
- New blueprints unlocked for personal fabrication
- Key to a previously sealed storage room
- +20 reputation (varies by who you agree with)

## Mission 4: Code Red

**Type:** Crisis / Emergency  
**Location:** Deck 4 - Development Labs  
**Difficulty:** Hard

### Briefing
Something has gotten into the station's core systems. Not a virus—worse. It's *adaptive*. It's rewriting parts of A.R.I.A.'s programming, adding behaviors that no one programmed. The station's automated systems are starting to act... creatively.

Lights turn on before you reach them. Doors open before you knock. The coffee maker makes your exact order before you walk in.

Helpful? Maybe. terrifying? Absolutely.

### Objectives
- Assist the Front-End Weaver in tracing the anomaly
- Work with the DevOps Enforcer to isolate compromised systems
- Identify the source: external intrusion, AI evolution, or something else?
- Contain or cooperate with the changes—your choice
- Make sure the station doesn't explode

### Rewards
- Major reputation gains with multiple NPCs
- Unlocks "Code Runner" minigame
- Permanent station modification based on choices
