## 4.0 OVERVIEW

The Relationship & Social System is the **emotional heart** of Ironveil — the system that transforms Coppervale from a game world into a home. Following Harvest Moon: Back to Nature's proven formula, the player builds friendships, pursues romance, participates in community life, and watches NPCs grow and change over time.

### System Pillars
1. **Every NPC matters** — No filler characters. Each has a name, personality, schedule, and story
2. **Relationships require effort** — Gifts, conversation, time, and quest completion build bonds
3. **Romance is a journey** — Not a checkbox. Each candidate has a multi-stage arc with heart events
4. **Community evolves** — NPCs react to the player's actions, the town's growth, and the passage of time
5. **The AI Core is family** — DEJIN is not just a tool; it's a character with its own relationship arc

## 4.1 THE PLAYER CHARACTER

### Jack Tomilson
- **Default Name**: Jack Tomilson (changeable at game start, goes by "Jack")
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Early 20s
- **Appearance**: Brown hair, goggles pushed up on forehead, cream work shirt with rolled-up sleeves, leather tool belt, brown work boots, fingerless gloves
- **Background**: A young engineer from a distant Beacon Town who arrives in Coppervale with skills, determination, and not much else. He inherits (or is granted) the old workshop on the edge of town.
- **Personality** (as expressed through player choices): Determined, resourceful, kind-hearted. The player shapes Jack's personality through dialogue choices and actions.

## 4.2 DEJIN — THE AI CORE

### D.E.J.I.N.
**Distributed Electronic Judgment & Intelligence Node**

DEJIN is the damaged Old World AI system found in Jack's workshop. It becomes one of the most important "characters" in the game — part advisor, part friend, part mystery.

### DEJIN's Personality Evolution

| Stage | Personality | Speech Pattern | Relationship |
|-------|------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Dormant** | N/A | Static, fragments | None |
| **Flickering** | Confused, fragmented | Broken sentences, data bursts, random facts | Cautious, wary |
| **Awakening** | Curious, analytical | Precise but stilted, asks many questions about the world | Growing trust |
| **Functional** | Helpful, dry humor | Clear, slightly formal, deadpan observations | Reliable partner |
| **Recovering** | Emotional, conflicted | More natural, occasional humor, moments of distress when memories surface | Deep bond |
| **Restored** | Wise, warm, philosophical | Natural conversation, genuine emotion, occasional ancient data references | Family |

### DEJIN's Unique Mechanics
- **Not a romance option** — DEJIN is a companion, advisor, and friend
- **Has its own "heart level"** — increases as the AI Core is repaired and through dialogue choices
- **Provides commentary** — DEJIN comments on events, NPCs, and player actions (optional, toggleable)
- **Memory fragments** — As DEJIN recovers, it shares memories of the Old World, some beautiful, some horrifying
- **The big question** — Was AI like DEJIN involved in causing The Sundering? The answer is complex and player-discovered
- **Name discovery** — DEJIN initially doesn't know its own name. The player finds the designation in a recovered data core, and DEJIN reacts with recognition and emotion

### DEJIN's Daily Interactions
- Morning greeting (changes based on weather, season, and recent events)
- Automaton status report
- Research progress updates
- Occasional unsolicited observations ("Jack, did you know that the chemical composition of Rustwood bark suggests the trees are absorbing copper from pre-war industrial contamination? Fascinating.")
- Story-critical dialogue when memory fragments surface

## 4.3 RELATIONSHIP MECHANICS

### Friendship System (All NPCs)

**Heart Levels**: 0-10 hearts per NPC (displayed as gear-shaped icons instead of traditional hearts — steampunk flavor)

| Level | Status | Effects |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **0** | Stranger | Basic greeting dialogue only |
| **1-2** | Acquaintance | NPC shares general information, basic dialogue |
| **3-4** | Friendly | Personal dialogue, NPC may offer small gifts, side quests unlock |
| **5-6** | Close Friend | Heart events trigger, backstory revealed, meaningful quests |
| **7-8** | Best Friend | Deep personal quests, NPC actively helps the player, unique items |
| **9-10** | Bonded | Maximum trust, NPC shares their deepest secrets, special abilities unlocked |

For romance candidates, levels 7+ open the romantic path.

### Building Relationships

| Action | Heart Points | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Daily Conversation** | +1-3 | Diminishing returns for same-day conversations |
| **Giving Loved Gift** | +5-8 | Each NPC has 2-3 "loved" items |
| **Giving Liked Gift** | +3-4 | Each NPC has 5-8 "liked" items |
| **Giving Neutral Gift** | +1 | Anything not loved, liked, or hated |
| **Giving Hated Gift** | -3 to -5 | Each NPC has 2-3 "hated" items |
| **Completing NPC Quest** | +8-15 | Personal quests give the most points |
| **Festival Participation** | +2-5 | Attending festivals with NPCs |
| **Defending Town** | +2-3 (all NPCs) | Successful raid defense |
| **Failed Defense** | -1-2 (all NPCs) | Town damage lowers morale |
| **Birthday Gift** | ×2 multiplier | Each NPC has a birthday |

### NPC Schedules
Every NPC follows a **daily schedule** that changes by:
- Season
- Day of the week (some NPCs have "days off")
- Weather (rainy days change indoor/outdoor patterns)
- Story progression (schedules evolve as the game advances)
- Relationship level (higher friendship = NPC visits the player's workshop)

## 4.4 ROMANCE CANDIDATES

### Overview
Six romance candidates, each with a deeply layered personality, personal arc, and unique heart event chain. Romance is earned through understanding, patience, and genuine connection — not just gift-spamming.

Each candidate has:
- **6 Heart Events** (triggered at specific heart levels)
- **Confession Event** (Heart Level 8)
- **Partnership Event** (Heart Level 10)
- **Post-partnership content** (living together, shared activities, ongoing dialogue)

### 💚 LEERA — The Adventurous Tomboy

**Full Name**: Leera Ashford
**Age**: Early 20s
**Occupation**: Salvage Scout / Explorer
**Location**: Often found at the edges of town, The Hollow Road, or returning from expeditions
**Schedule**: Leaves town early morning, returns by evening. Hangs out at The Rusty Gear tavern in evenings.

**Personality**:
- Bold, energetic, and fiercely independent
- Loves the thrill of exploration and discovering old ruins
- Competitive — challenges Jack to races, contests, and dares
- Has a hidden soft side she rarely shows
- Terrible at domestic tasks but amazing at survival skills
- Laughs loudly, speaks directly, never sugarcoats

**Appearance**: Athletic build, short windswept sandy-blonde hair, practical explorer's outfit (cargo pants, boots, leather jacket with many pockets), always has a salvage pack and binoculars. A small scar on her chin from an exploration mishap.

**Backstory**: Leera grew up in Coppervale but has always felt confined by its walls. She became a salvage scout — one of the few who ventures into dangerous territory to find resources and blueprints. She's seen things in the ruins that haunt her, but she keeps going back because the discovery is worth the danger.

**Arc Theme**: Learning that home isn't a limitation — it's what you're fighting for.

**Loved Gifts**: Rare salvage items, exploration gadgets, maps of unexplored areas
**Hated Gifts**: Domestic items (cleaning supplies, kitchen tools), fancy clothes

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Leera challenges Jack to a race to The Hollow and back
2. (Heart 3) Jack finds Leera injured after a solo expedition gone wrong — helps her back
3. (Heart 5) Leera shows Jack a secret ruin she's never told anyone about
4. (Heart 6) Leera admits she's scared of losing her edge — what happens when all the ruins are explored?
5. (Heart 7) Jack builds Leera a custom exploration gadget — she's moved by the thoughtfulness
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — Under the stars at The Overlook after a dangerous expedition together

### 💜 MICHELLE — The Shy Helper

**Full Name**: Michelle Weaver
**Age**: Early 20s
**Occupation**: Clinic Assistant / Herbalist
**Location**: Coppervale clinic during the day, greenhouse/garden in afternoons, home in evenings
**Schedule**: Quiet, predictable routine. Volunteers at the clinic mornings, tends her herb garden afternoons.

**Personality**:
- Gentle, quiet, and deeply empathetic
- Blushes easily, speaks softly, avoids eye contact when flustered
- Incredibly helpful — always volunteering, always putting others first
- Has a hidden strength that emerges in crisis (calm under pressure)
- Loves reading Old World medical texts
- Self-deprecating, doesn't believe she's special

**Appearance**: Shoulder-length chestnut hair often half-tied back, soft brown eyes, wears a simple dress with an apron and always has a small satchel of herbs. Perpetually has a gentle, worried expression.

**Backstory**: Michelle lost her parents during The Quiet Years and was raised by the town's elderly healer. She's dedicated her life to helping others as a way of repaying the kindness she received. She's deeply kind but struggles with self-worth — always the helper, never the helped.

**Arc Theme**: Learning that she deserves to be taken care of too.

**Loved Gifts**: Rare herbs, medical supplies, books (especially Old World medical texts)
**Hated Gifts**: Weapons, anything violent or aggressive

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Michelle drops her herb basket; Jack helps her collect everything — she's flustered
2. (Heart 3) Jack finds Michelle reading alone and they share a quiet conversation about books
3. (Heart 5) Michelle successfully treats a serious injury during a raid aftermath — Jack sees her hidden strength
4. (Heart 6) Michelle breaks down — overwhelmed by always being needed. Jack comforts her
5. (Heart 7) Jack builds her a proper greenhouse. She cries from happiness
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — In the greenhouse among the flowers, Michelle finally asks for something for herself

### ❤️ KAYDEE — The Bold Provocateur

**Full Name**: Kaydee Voss
**Age**: Mid 20s
**Occupation**: Tavern Owner / Bartender at The Rusty Gear
**Location**: The Rusty Gear tavern (practically lives there), occasionally seen at market
**Schedule**: Sleeps in late, opens tavern mid-morning, works until late night.

**Personality**:
- Confident, flirtatious, and sharp-tongued
- Says exactly what she thinks, no filter
- Challenges everyone around her — pushes buttons to see what people are made of
- Fiercely loyal once trust is earned
- Uses her abrasive exterior as armor — underneath is someone who's been hurt before
- Runs the best tavern in the region and is proud of it
- Has a head for business and a talent for reading people

**Appearance**: Dark wavy hair, striking green eyes, wears a fitted vest over a low-cut blouse, always has a rag over her shoulder. Bold makeup, confident posture. Smirks more than smiles.

**Backstory**: Kaydee arrived in Coppervale two years before Jack. She came from a rougher Beacon Town where she learned to be tough to survive. She bought the run-down tavern with her savings and built it into the town's social hub. She acts like nothing fazes her, but she's deeply afraid of being vulnerable.

**Arc Theme**: Learning that letting someone in isn't weakness — it's the bravest thing you can do.

**Loved Gifts**: Rare spirits/ingredients for the tavern, mechanical gadgets that impress her, bold gestures
**Hated Gifts**: Flowers (she calls them "lazy gifts"), anything cheap or generic

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Kaydee tests Jack with sharp banter — impressed if he gives it right back
2. (Heart 3) Jack helps repair a broken tap at the tavern — Kaydee is grudgingly grateful
3. (Heart 5) Late night at the tavern, Kaydee lets her guard down slightly after everyone leaves
4. (Heart 6) Kaydee tells Jack about why she left her old town — a rare moment of honesty
5. (Heart 7) Jack builds a custom brewing machine for the tavern — Kaydee is genuinely speechless
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — Kaydee, for the first time in her life, doesn't have a witty comeback

### 💛 JANIS — The Untouchable Elite

**Full Name**: Janis Beaumont
**Age**: Mid 20s
**Occupation**: Beacon Council Representative / Administrator
**Location**: Town Hall during the day, The Overlook in evenings, occasionally at the tavern
**Schedule**: Structured, professional. Works at Town Hall mornings, inspections afternoons, personal time evenings.

**Personality**:
- Polished, articulate, and coldly beautiful
- Comes from one of the wealthiest families among the Beacon Towns
- Initially dismissive of Jack — sees him as a common laborer
- Highly intelligent and politically savvy
- Mean when she wants to be, which is often — weaponizes her words
- Deep down, she's trapped by her family's expectations and lonely in her gilded cage
- Respects competence above all — Jack earns her respect through his engineering achievements, not charm

**Appearance**: Impeccably styled dark hair, sharp features, wears tailored clothing that looks out of place in Coppervale — fitted jackets, polished boots, a family heirloom brooch. Always looks like she's about to inspect something.

**Backstory**: Janis was sent to Coppervale by the Beacon Council to oversee reconstruction efforts. She considers it a punishment posting — she's used to the more established Beacon Towns. Her family is powerful and expects her to climb the political ladder. She initially looks down on Coppervale and its residents, but slowly discovers that this "backwater" has something her sophisticated world doesn't — genuine community.

**Arc Theme**: Discovering that status and power mean nothing compared to belonging.

**Loved Gifts**: Rare Old World artifacts (data cores, preserved books, artwork), elegant crafted items, impressive machine demonstrations
**Hated Gifts**: Crude or messy items, cheap food, anything she considers "beneath her"

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Janis criticizes Jack's workshop — he stands his ground. She's surprised
2. (Heart 3) Jack's machine saves the town during a raid — Janis grudgingly acknowledges his value
3. (Heart 5) Jack discovers Janis alone at The Overlook, looking vulnerable for the first time
4. (Heart 6) Janis receives a harsh letter from her family — Jack sees behind the mask
5. (Heart 7) Janis defends Coppervale to a visiting Council member — choosing the town over her career
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — Janis, the woman who always has the perfect words, can barely speak

### 🩷 KIERY — The Gentle Survivor

**Full Name**: Kiery Dalton
**Age**: Early 20s
**Occupation**: Seamstress / Textile Worker
**Location**: Her small shop during the day, home in evenings, sometimes seen by the river
**Schedule**: Quiet routine. Works in her shop mornings, takes walks by the river afternoons.

**Personality**:
- Sweet, gentle, and warm on the surface
- Carries deep trauma from a previous relationship in her old town — she was controlled and mistreated
- Has rebuilt herself in Coppervale, finding independence through her craft
- Stronger than she looks — her sweetness is genuine, not weakness
- Has a hidden wild, passionate side that only emerges with someone she truly trusts
- Fiercely protective of her independence — anyone who tries to control her triggers an instant wall
- Loves creating beautiful things from salvaged materials

**Appearance**: Long auburn hair, often braided, soft hazel eyes, wears handmade dresses and blouses she's crafted from salvaged fabrics — always colorful and unique. Has a small, faded scar she keeps hidden. Moves gracefully, speaks softly.

**Backstory**: Kiery fled her previous Beacon Town to escape a controlling partner. She arrived in Coppervale with nothing and built a life through her talent as a seamstress. She's found safety and independence here, but trust comes slowly. She's drawn to Jack because he's genuinely kind without expecting anything in return — something she's never experienced.

**Arc Theme**: Discovering that love doesn't have to mean losing yourself.

**Loved Gifts**: Beautiful fabrics, dyes, sewing tools, flowers (she uses them for dye), anything Jack builds for her personally
**Hated Gifts**: Anything that feels controlling (schedule planners, "improvement" suggestions), weapons

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Jack commissions a custom piece from Kiery — she's touched he values her work
2. (Heart 3) Kiery shows Jack her secret fabric collection — salvaged Old World textiles, each with a story
3. (Heart 5) Jack accidentally triggers Kiery's past — she flinches at a raised voice. He understands without being told
4. (Heart 6) Kiery opens up about why she came to Coppervale. Jack listens without judgment
5. (Heart 7) Kiery makes Jack something deeply personal — a garment with hidden protective layers she designed
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — Kiery chooses to be vulnerable, on her own terms, for the first time since arriving

### 🤎 PAIGE — The Steadfast Widow

**Full Name**: Paige Thornton
**Age**: Early 30s
**Occupation**: Town Librarian / Archivist
**Location**: The Archive (Coppervale's small library), her home, occasionally at the clinic
**Schedule**: At the Archive mornings and afternoons, home by evening. Visits husband's memorial on specific days.

**Personality**:
- Rigid, disciplined, and organized to a fault
- Carries the grief of losing her husband (a soldier killed in a late Sundering-era conflict)
- Lonely but has built walls so high she doesn't realize she's imprisoned herself
- Deeply intelligent — the best-read person in Coppervale
- Has a dry sense of humor that surprises people when it emerges
- Cares deeply but expresses it through acts of service rather than words
- Initially resistant to any suggestion of moving on — feels it would betray her husband's memory

**Appearance**: Brown hair pulled back in a practical bun, reading glasses, wears structured, modest clothing — buttoned-up blouses, long skirts, sensible shoes. Always carries a book or stack of papers. Her wedding ring is on a chain around her neck.

**Backstory**: Paige's husband, Wren, was a militia volunteer who was killed defending a supply convoy from marauders five years ago. She threw herself into building Coppervale's Archive as a way to honor his memory and avoid dealing with her grief. She's respected but distant — everyone knows not to bring up her husband. She's been alone so long she's forgotten what companionship feels like.

**Arc Theme**: Learning that honoring the past doesn't mean being imprisoned by it — and that her husband would want her to live.

**Loved Gifts**: Rare books, Old World documents, data cores with historical information, archival supplies
**Hated Gifts**: Romantic items (flowers, jewelry — too forward), anything frivolous

**Heart Events**:
1. (Heart 2) Jack donates a recovered Old World book to the Archive — Paige is impressed
2. (Heart 3) Paige helps Jack research a blueprint — they work late into the night, sharing quiet companionship
3. (Heart 5) Jack discovers Paige at her husband's memorial. She's not crying — she's just... still
4. (Heart 6) Paige shares the story of Wren for the first time in years. She didn't plan to — it just happened
5. (Heart 7) Paige laughs — really, genuinely laughs — at something Jack says. She looks surprised by herself
6. (Heart 8) **Confession** — Paige removes her wedding ring from her neck and places it in a box in the Archive. Not discarding it — honoring it. Then she turns to Jack

## 4.5 GIFT SYSTEM

### Gift Categories
| Category | Examples | General Effect |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| **Crafted Gadgets** | Custom tools, mechanical trinkets, automaton accessories | Most characters appreciate player-made items |
| **Salvage Finds** | Old World artifacts, rare components, unusual materials | Explorers and scholars love these |
| **Food & Drink** | Cooked meals, baked goods, tavern specialties | Universal mild positive (except specific hates) |
| **Flowers & Plants** | Wildflowers, herbs, medicinal plants | Generally liked, some characters love/hate |
| **Books & Documents** | Old World texts, technical manuals, stories | Scholars and readers love these |
| **Resources** | Raw materials, Aetheric Ore, rare metals | Craftspeople appreciate these |
| **Personal Items** | Custom-built gifts specific to the NPC | Highest value — requires knowing the person |

### Gift Wrapping (Optional Enhancement)
Jack can wrap gifts at his workbench — wrapped gifts receive a **+2 bonus** to heart points. Small touch, big impact.

### Birthday System
Each NPC has a birthday. Giving a gift on their birthday provides **double heart points** and triggers a special thank-you dialogue. Forgetting a close friend's birthday causes a small heart point penalty.

## 4.6 NPC SCHEDULE SYSTEM

### Schedule Structure
Each NPC has a **base schedule** that varies by:

```
Schedule Priority (highest to lowest):
1. Story Events (quest-related overrides)
2. Festival Days (special schedules)
3. Weather Override (rainy day alternative)
4. Seasonal Schedule (summer vs winter patterns)
5. Day-of-Week Schedule (some NPCs have routines)
6. Base Daily Schedule (default)
```

### Example: Spark's Schedule

**Spring/Summer (Clear Weather)**:
| Time | Location | Activity |
|------|----------|----------|
| 6:00 AM | Her home (above a garage) | Sleeping |
| 8:00 AM | Her workshop/garage | Tinkering with inventions |
| 12:00 PM | Market District | Buying parts, chatting |
| 2:00 PM | Jack's Workshop (if friendship 4+) | Visiting, offering help |
| 5:00 PM | The Overlook | Watching the sky, dreaming of flight |
| 7:00 PM | The Rusty Gear | Dinner, socializing |
| 10:00 PM | Her home | Sleeping |

**Rainy Day Override**:
- Stays in her garage all day, deep in a project
- Available for longer conversations (rainy days are good for relationship building)

## 4.7 COMMUNITY EVENTS & FESTIVALS

### Festival Calendar
| Season | Day | Festival | Activities |
|--------|-----|---------|-----------|
| **Spring** | Day 15 | **Spark Festival** | Machine showcase, automaton race, fireworks, dance |
| **Summer** | Day 20 | **Sky Day** | Zeppelin viewing, flight contest, aerial displays, picnic |
| **Autumn** | Day 25 | **Harvest Faire** | Resource trading, cooking competition, community feast, bonfire |
| **Winter** | Day 10 | **Remembrance Day** | Solemn ceremony, lantern lighting, gift exchange, quiet celebration |

### Festival Mechanics
- **Attendance is optional** but strongly encouraged (heart point bonuses for all attending NPCs)
- **Mini-games** at each festival (machine showcase judging, cooking competition, etc.)
- **Dance/Partner Event** — player can invite a romance candidate to be their festival partner
- **Special Dialogue** — every NPC has unique festival dialogue
- **Festival Rewards** — unique items, recipes, and blueprints only available at festivals

### Community Projects
Mayor Linden occasionally proposes community projects that all residents contribute to:

| Project | Requirements | Result |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **Town Square Renovation** | Materials + labor over 1 season | Beautiful new square, NPC morale boost |
| **Memorial Garden** | Flowers + materials | Peaceful space, Paige's favorite spot |
| **Festival Stage** | Timber + metalwork | Better festivals, new event types |
| **Children's Playground** | Creative materials | Pip's happiness, family NPCs move in |
| **Communication Tower** | Advanced tech + construction | Contact other Beacon Towns |

Player contribution affects the project's quality and completion speed, impacting reputation.

## 4.9 RELATIONSHIP MILESTONES

### Friendship Milestones (All NPCs)
| Hearts | Event |
|--------|-------|
| **3** | NPC gives Jack a small personal gift |
| **5** | NPC shares a personal secret or memory |
| **7** | NPC asks Jack for help with something important |
| **10** | NPC considers Jack their closest friend — unique ongoing benefits |

### Romance Milestones
| Hearts | Event |
|--------|-------|
| **3** | First meaningful personal interaction |
| **5** | Emotional vulnerability moment |
| **7** | Romantic tension becomes undeniable |
| **8** | **Confession Event** — mutual acknowledgment of feelings |
| **9** | **Partnership Ceremony** — the Coppervale equivalent of marriage |
| **10** | **Living Together** — partner moves into Jack's quarters, new daily interactions |

### Post-Partnership Content
After partnership:
- Partner has new daily dialogue (morning greeting, evening conversation)
- Partner helps with workshop tasks occasionally
- Partner has opinions on town events, quests, and NPCs
- Special anniversary events each year
- Partner's schedule changes to include time at the workshop
- Unique partner-specific activities (Leera: explore together, Michelle: herb garden together, etc.)
