## 1.0 MUSICAL IDENTITY

### 1.1 The Sonic Soul of Ironveil

Ironveil's music must accomplish the same trick as its art: **70% warmth, 30% grit**. The player should feel like they're in a cozy, handcrafted world — but one built on the bones of something vast and broken. The music is nostalgic without being sad, hopeful without being naive, and warm without ignoring the cold.

**The Formula**: Acoustic folk warmth + light orchestral sweep + steampunk industrial texture

### 1.2 Core Instrument Palette

| Instrument | Role | When Used |
|------------|------|-----------|
| **Acoustic Guitar** | Warmth, home, daily life | Town themes, morning, relationships |
| **Piano** | Emotion, elegance, introspection | Heart events, DEJIN scenes, quiet moments |
| **Accordion/Concertina** | Steampunk flavor, European folk warmth | Market, festivals, lighthearted moments |
| **Strings (Violin, Cello)** | Sweeping emotion, grandeur, tension | Story moments, exploration vistas, combat build-up |
| **Gentle Woodwinds (Flute, Clarinet)** | Pastoral beauty, nature, peace | Spring/summer outdoor scenes, morning |
| **Metallic Percussion** | Steampunk texture, industry, rhythm | Workshop, building, mechanical moments |
| **Anvil Strikes** | Punctuation, forge rhythm | Workshop theme, crafting sequences |
| **Gear Clicks & Steam Hisses** | Ambient steampunk texture | Woven subtly into workshop and town themes |
| **Music Box / Celesta** | Nostalgia, Old World memory, mystery | DEJIN memory scenes, lore discoveries |
| **Brass (French Horn, Trumpet)** | Military, authority, epic moments | Combat, The Marshal's theme, triumphant moments |
| **War Drums / Taiko** | Urgency, threat, power | Raid themes, siege, boss encounters |
| **Choir (Wordless)** | Awe, the sacred, revelation | Grand Spire, Sundering revelations, climactic moments |

### 1.3 Musical Rules

1. **No synths or electronic instruments** — everything should sound organic, handcrafted, analog (matching the steampunk aesthetic of hand-built technology)
2. **Metallic percussion is texture, not lead** — gear clicks, anvil taps, and steam hisses are woven into tracks as subtle rhythmic elements, never overpowering
3. **Silence is a tool** — some moments (dawn, the workshop before work, DEJIN's heaviest revelations) benefit from near-silence with only ambient sound
4. **Leitmotifs matter** — recurring character and location themes create emotional continuity (see Section 2.5)
5. **Dynamic layering** — tracks should be composed in layers that can be added/removed based on context (e.g., raid music adds drums as waves progress)

### 1.4 Mood Reference Points

| Reference | What to Take |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Stardew Valley OST (ConcernedApe)** | Warmth, seasonal variety, the "home" feeling |
| **Bastion OST (Darren Korb)** | Post-apocalyptic beauty, acoustic + industrial blend |
| **Spirited Away OST (Joe Hisaishi)** | Emotional depth, wonder, melancholy-tinged joy |
| **Hollow Knight OST (Christopher Larkin)** | Atmospheric exploration, sense of place |
| **Ori and the Blind Forest (Gareth Coker)** | Emotional swells, nature themes, hope through adversity |

## 2.0 MUSIC TRACK LIST

### 2.1 Main Themes

#### "Ironveil" — Main Theme
**When**: Title screen, key story moments, end credits
**Mood**: Hopeful determination. The feeling of looking at a broken world and choosing to rebuild it.
**Structure**: Begins with a solo acoustic guitar playing a simple, memorable melody. Piano joins. Strings swell. The melody builds to a full orchestral statement with warm brass — not triumphant, but resolute. Metallic percussion (gentle anvil taps) provides subtle steampunk texture. Ends with the guitar alone again, the melody now carrying the weight of everything that came before.
**Duration**: ~3:00 (title version), shorter motif versions for in-game callbacks
**Key**: D major (warm, open, hopeful)

#### "Coppervale" — Town Theme
**When**: Overworld in Coppervale during daytime
**Mood**: Home. Community. The smell of bread and the sound of hammers. Safe, warm, alive.
**Structure**: Acoustic guitar and accordion carry a gentle, swaying melody. Clarinet adds a pastoral voice. Light percussion (brushed snare, woodblock) provides rhythm. Subtle gear-click texture in the background. The melody is catchy but never intrusive — something the player hums without realizing.
**Duration**: ~2:30 (loops seamlessly)
**Key**: G major
**Seasonal Variants**: See Section 2.2

#### "The Workshop" — Workshop Theme
**When**: Inside the player's workshop during building/crafting
**Mood**: Productive joy. The satisfaction of creating something with your hands. Focused but fun.
**Structure**: More rhythmic than the town theme. Anvil strikes and gear clicks form a steady industrial beat. Acoustic guitar plays a bouncy, syncopated melody. Accordion adds warmth. When the player is actively building, the music subtly intensifies — more percussion, faster tempo. When idle, it relaxes.
**Duration**: ~3:00 (loops seamlessly, with dynamic layers)
**Key**: A major (bright, energetic)

#### "The Overlook" — Quiet Theme
**When**: The Overlook hill, stargazing, romantic moments, quiet reflection
**Mood**: Peaceful contemplation. The beauty of a scarred world under an open sky.
**Structure**: Solo piano, later joined by a single cello. Minimal, spacious, breathing. No percussion. The melody is slow and tender, with pauses that let the ambient sounds (wind, distant town noise) fill the space.
**Duration**: ~2:30
**Key**: E-flat major (warm, intimate)

### 2.2 Seasonal Variants

Each main location theme has four seasonal arrangements. The melody remains the same, but instrumentation, tempo, and mood shift:

#### Coppervale — Seasonal Variants

| Season | Changes | Mood Shift |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Spring** | Add flute melody line, birdsong-like ornaments, lighter percussion. Slightly faster tempo. | Fresh, hopeful, new beginnings |
| **Summer** | Fuller arrangement — add warm strings, stronger rhythm, bright acoustic guitar strumming. Peak energy. | Energetic, productive, golden |
| **Autumn** | Accordion takes lead melody. Add cello warmth. Slightly slower tempo. Minor-key bridge section. Falling-leaf wind chime texture. | Warm, reflective, bittersweet beauty |
| **Winter** | Piano takes lead. Reduce percussion to near-nothing. Add subtle music box texture. Strings provide a warm blanket of sound. Most intimate arrangement. | Cozy, quiet, contemplative |

#### Workshop — Seasonal Variants

| Season | Changes |
|--------|---------|
| **Spring** | Standard arrangement — bright and productive |
| **Summer** | Faster tempo, more percussion, added brass accents — peak building season |
| **Autumn** | Slightly moodier — minor key moments creep in. The stakes are higher. |
| **Winter** | Warmest arrangement — the workshop is a refuge. Emphasis on interior coziness against the cold outside. |

### 2.3 Exploration Themes

Each explorable region has its own theme reflecting its unique environment and emotional tone:

#### "The Hollow" — Verdant Basin Exploration
**Mood**: Wonder tinged with melancholy. A beautiful scar.
**Instruments**: Solo flute over ambient strings. Gentle harp arpeggios. Distant, reverb-heavy piano notes. Water sounds woven into the music.
**Key**: C major → A minor (shifts between wonder and sadness)
**Duration**: ~3:00

#### "Rustwood" — Forest Exploration
**Mood**: Eerie beauty. Nature reclaiming industry. Something still running in the dark.
**Instruments**: Muted cello melody. Distant metallic resonance (like tapping on old pipes). Woodwind whispers. Occasional gear-click percussion — the sound of machines still turning. Low strings provide unease.
**Key**: D minor (mysterious, unsettled)
**Duration**: ~3:30

#### "Ashspine" — Mountain Exploration
**Mood**: Grandeur and isolation. Cold, vast, ancient.
**Instruments**: French horn carries a wide, sweeping melody. Strings provide a bed of sustained chords. Wind sounds. Sparse percussion — deep, resonant drums like distant thunder. Flute echoes off imaginary stone walls.
**Key**: B-flat major → G minor
**Duration**: ~3:00

#### "Shattered Coast" — Coastal Exploration
**Mood**: Salty melancholy. Lost glory. The sea doesn't care about your wars.
**Instruments**: Accordion plays a sailor's melody. Acoustic guitar provides rhythm. Distant ship-bell percussion. Strings swell like waves. Seagull-like flute ornaments.
**Key**: F major (open, maritime)
**Duration**: ~3:00

#### "Scorchlands" — Wasteland Exploration
**Mood**: Desolation with a pulse. This place was murdered — but something still breathes.
**Instruments**: Sparse. A lone cello plays a mournful melody. Metallic percussion — hollow, distant, like wind through wreckage. Droning low strings. Occasional brass stabs. The most austere and unsettling track.
**Key**: F minor (bleak, heavy)
**Duration**: ~3:30

#### "Spire Wastes" — City Ruins Exploration
**Mood**: Awe and sorrow. The ghost of a great civilization.
**Instruments**: Full orchestra at its most restrained. Wordless choir provides a haunting backdrop. Piano plays fragmented phrases — like trying to remember a song. Strings carry the weight. Music box textures reference the Old World's lost beauty.
**Key**: E-flat minor → E-flat major (darkness to light as discoveries are made)
**Duration**: ~4:00

#### "The Grand Spire" — Interior Exploration
**Mood**: Revelation. Each floor brings you closer to the truth.
**Instruments**: Builds progressively as the player ascends. Lower floors: ambient textures, distant piano, silence. Mid floors: strings enter, building tension. Upper floors: full orchestra, restrained but powerful. The Apex: the main theme melody returns, recontextualized with choir and full strings — the most emotionally overwhelming musical moment in the game.
**Key**: Modulates upward as the player ascends — C minor → D minor → E minor → F major (resolution)
**Duration**: ~5:00 (segmented by floor, dynamic)

### 2.4 Combat & Raid Themes

#### "They Come" — Raid Preparation
**When**: Intel phase, building defenses before a raid
**Mood**: Tense anticipation. The storm is coming.
**Instruments**: Low strings tremolo. Distant war drums. Metallic percussion builds slowly. The town theme melody appears briefly in minor key — a reminder of what's at stake.
**Key**: D minor
**Duration**: ~2:00 (loops)

#### "Hold the Line" — Standard Raid Combat
**When**: Active raid combat (Freelance Raiders, Rust Wolves)
**Mood**: Urgent but not hopeless. Defend what you've built.
**Instruments**: Driving percussion (snare rolls, taiko accents). Aggressive cello and low strings. Brass stabs on enemy wave arrivals. Acoustic guitar provides a human warmth underneath — this isn't a war movie, it's neighbors defending their home.
**Dynamic Layers**:
- Layer 1 (Base): Percussion + low strings — plays throughout
- Layer 2 (Escalation): Brass + higher strings — added as wave intensity increases
- Layer 3 (Crisis): Full orchestra + choir accent — added when defenses are breached or player health is critical
**Key**: A minor
**Duration**: ~4:00 (loops with dynamic layers)

#### "Iron March" — Iron Marauder Raids
**When**: Iron Marauder attacks (Year 1 Winter onward)
**Mood**: Organized menace. A real army, not bandits.
**Instruments**: Military snare drum drives the rhythm. Brass plays a cold, disciplined march melody. Strings provide urgency. The Marshal's leitmotif (see 2.5) appears in the brass. More structured and ominous than "Hold the Line."
**Key**: C minor
**Duration**: ~4:00 (loops)

#### "The Siege" — Major Siege Events
**When**: Year 2 Winter siege, major story raids
**Mood**: Epic, desperate, everything on the line.
**Instruments**: Full orchestra. War drums. Choir. The main theme melody appears fragmented in the brass — fighting to survive. Dynamic layers escalate across waves. Between waves, the music drops to tense strings and distant drums, then explodes back for the next assault.
**Key**: D minor → D major (if victory is achieved — the resolution is triumphant)
**Duration**: ~6:00 (segmented across waves)

#### "Mech Deployed" — Player Mech Combat
**When**: Player switches to direct mech control during a raid
**Mood**: Power. You are the weapon now.
**Instruments**: Heavy percussion. Driving bass. Brass plays a heroic, weighty melody. Gear-click and steam-hiss texture at maximum. The most "steampunk" track in the game. Should feel like piloting a walking tank built by hand.
**Key**: E minor (powerful, dark-edged)
**Duration**: ~3:00 (loops)

#### "The Marshal" — Boss Encounter
**When**: Direct confrontation with The Marshal (Year 2 Winter, Year 3 Autumn)
**Mood**: Respectful adversary. This is not a monster — it's a man who chose the wrong path.
**Instruments**: The Marshal's leitmotif (see 2.5) played in full by brass and strings. War drums provide a relentless heartbeat. Choir enters during the most intense moments. Piano plays a counter-melody — DEJIN's theme, representing the philosophical conflict underneath the physical one.
**Key**: C minor → resolution varies by ending
**Duration**: ~5:00

### 2.6 Festival Music

#### "Spark Festival" — Spring
**Mood**: Joyful celebration. Fireworks and machine showcases.
**Instruments**: Upbeat accordion and fiddle lead a fast, danceable tune. Tambourine and handclaps. Gear-click percussion adds steampunk flavor. Brass fanfares for machine showcase moments.
**Key**: G major
**Duration**: ~2:30

#### "Sky Day" — Summer
**Mood**: Wonder and freedom. Looking up.
**Instruments**: Soaring flute melody. Strings provide lift. Harp arpeggios shimmer. Lighter than Spark Festival — more airy, more spacious. Spark's leitmotif appears.
**Key**: D major
**Duration**: ~2:30

#### "Harvest Faire" — Autumn
**Mood**: Warm community gathering. Gratitude and togetherness.
**Instruments**: Acoustic guitar and accordion play a warm, swaying waltz. Cello adds richness. Gentle percussion (woodblock, shaker). The most "folk music" track in the game. Slightly bittersweet in the bridge — autumn is beautiful but fleeting.
**Key**: A major → F-sharp minor (bridge) → A major
**Duration**: ~3:00

#### "Remembrance" — Winter
**Mood**: Solemn respect. Grief honored, not wallowed in.
**Instruments**: Solo cello plays a slow, dignified melody. Piano provides gentle harmonic support. No percussion. The melody is simple and repeatable — like a hymn. At the lantern-lighting moment, wordless choir enters softly. The Sundering leitmotif appears briefly, then resolves into Hope Rekindled. Tears and smiles.
**Key**: E-flat minor → E-flat major
**Duration**: ~3:00

### 2.7 Emotional/Story Themes

#### "Old World" — Memory Scenes
**When**: DEJIN memory recoveries, Old World flashbacks
**Mood**: Bittersweet nostalgia. Beauty lost.
**Instruments**: Music box plays DEJIN's leitmotif. Distant, reverbed strings. Piano fragments. Like hearing a song through water — present but unreachable.
**Key**: F major (warm but distant)
**Duration**: ~2:00

#### "The Weight" — Heavy Revelations
**When**: Sundering truth reveals, moral dilemmas, The Marshal's backstory
**Mood**: The gravity of history. No easy answers.
**Instruments**: Low strings, sustained chords. Solo cello. The Sundering leitmotif. Silence between phrases. The most restrained, most emotionally heavy track.
**Key**: C minor
**Duration**: ~2:30

#### "Heart to Heart" — Romance Scenes
**When**: Heart events, confessions, partnership ceremonies
**Mood**: Vulnerable intimacy. Two people choosing each other.
**Instruments**: Piano and cello duet (the Love Theme leitmotif). Acoustic guitar joins for warmth. No percussion — just breath and melody. Each romance candidate's confession scene can have a subtle variation (Leera's is slightly more adventurous, Paige's more solemn, Kaydee's more tentative).
**Key**: Various — shifts to match the emotional color of each candidate
**Duration**: ~2:00

#### "Victory" — Post-Battle Triumph
**When**: After successful raid defense, major story victories
**Mood**: Relief and pride. We did it. We're still here.
**Instruments**: The main theme melody returns in full — acoustic guitar, then strings, then brass. Hope Rekindled leitmotif soars. Percussion is celebratory (cymbals, bright snare). The warmest, most uplifting version of the main theme.
**Key**: D major
**Duration**: ~1:30

#### "The Apex" — Climactic Revelation
**When**: The Grand Spire summit, the final truth, the panoramic vista
**Mood**: Transcendent. Everything the player has built, fought for, and learned — condensed into a single moment of clarity.
**Instruments**: Full orchestra. Wordless choir. The main theme melody played by every instrument in unison, then breaking into harmony. DEJIN's leitmotif weaves through. The Sundering leitmotif appears one last time, then resolves permanently into Hope Rekindled. The most emotionally overwhelming piece in the game.
**Key**: D minor → D major (the entire game's emotional arc in one modulation)
**Duration**: ~4:00

## 4.0 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### 4.1 Audio System Architecture (per GDD Section 6)

| Channel | Content | Behavior |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Music** | One track at a time | 2-second crossfade between tracks |
| **Ambient** | Location soundscape | Loops, crossfades on location change |
| **SFX** | One-shot sounds | Priority-based (combat > workshop > environmental) |
| **UI** | Interface sounds | Highest priority, never ducked |
| **Dialogue** | NPC vocal clips | Plays during text display, queued |

### 4.2 Dynamic Music Rules

| Trigger | Music Response |
|---------|---------------|
| Enter town from outdoors | Crossfade to town theme (seasonal variant) |
| Enter workshop | Crossfade to workshop theme |
| Enter interior (NPC home/shop) | Reduce town theme volume, add interior ambience |
| Begin exploration zone | Crossfade to region exploration theme |
| Raid intel received | Crossfade to "They Come" preparation theme |
| Raid begins | Hard cut to combat theme |
| Raid wave escalation | Add dynamic layers to combat theme |
| Deploy mech | Crossfade to "Mech Deployed" |
| Raid victory | Fanfare → crossfade to "Victory" → town theme |
| Heart event trigger | Crossfade to "Heart to Heart" |
| DEJIN memory scene | Crossfade to "Old World" |
| Major story revelation | Crossfade to "The Weight" |
| Festival begins | Crossfade to festival-specific theme |
| Dawn | Music volume dips slightly, birdsong ambient rises |
| Night | Music gains reverb, ambient shifts to night sounds |
| Time of day: late night | Music fades to near-silence, ambient dominates |

### 4.3 Sound Design Priorities

**Must Have for Prototype**:
