# Game Concept - Ironveil (Master Brief)

## Overview
Derek is building Ironveil from scratch. A post-apocalyptic steampunk rebuilding simulation in the style of Harvest Moon: Back to Nature.

## Technical Decisions
- **Engine**: GameMaker (GML)
- **Platforms**: PC (Windows, macOS, Linux) + Mobile (iOS, Android)
- **Mod Support**: None
- **Tile Size**: 16x16 pixels, 3x render scale
- **Art Style**: Chibi sprites, top-down 3/4 view, Harvest Moon meets steampunk

## Player Character
- **Name**: Jack Tomilson (default, changeable), goes by "Jack"
- **Gender**: Male

## AI Companion
- **Name**: DEJIN (Distributed Electronic Judgment & Intelligence Node)

## Romance Candidates (6 females)
- Leera Ashford, Michelle Weaver, Kaydee Voss, Janis Beaumont, Kiery Dalton, Paige Thornton

## Core Design Decisions
- 20 real min/day, 30 days/season, 4 seasons/year, 15-20 year playthrough
- Full game scope, all 7 regions explorable, 3+ years main story
- Manual first build, then automaton-assisted. Daily maintenance.
- Tower defense → hybrid with player-controlled mech
- Tone: Dark but getting lighter — player is the reason for hope
