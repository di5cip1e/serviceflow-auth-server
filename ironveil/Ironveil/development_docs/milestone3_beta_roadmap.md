## Phase A: Combat & Machines Core (Sessions 4-5)

### #32 — Mech Combat System [MEDIUM]
Player-controlled mech combat during raids.
- **Movement**: Weighty physics — low accel, momentum, wide turns
- **Weapons**: Primary (LMB: cannon/gatling), Secondary (RMB: missiles/flame, cooldown)
- **Stomp Attack**: Spacebar AoE, stuns small units
- **Special Abilities**: Q key — per-mech-type (Overdrive, Energy Shield, Siege Mode, Charge)
- **Component Damage**: 4 HP pools (Legs→Slow, Arms→Accuracy, Torso→Main HP, Cockpit→Eject)
- **View Switch**: Tab key toggles strategic ↔ mech camera instantly

### #31 — Full Raid System [LARGE]
Complete all 4 enemy factions, advanced raid mechanics, multi-year schedules.
- **4 Enemy Factions**:
  - Freelance Raiders (Tier 1) — already done
  - Rust Wolves (Tier 2) — Scout, Raider, Berserker, Sapper, Alpha Wolf boss
  - Iron Marauders (Tier 3) — Infantry, Shieldbearer, Gunner, Technician, Iron Mech boss, Siege Ram, Lieutenant, The Marshal
  - Tide Reavers (West-only, Tier 2) — Raider, Diver (bypasses walls via water), Captain boss
- **Advanced Mechanics**: Multi-direction raids, Night/Storm/Fog/Coordinated modifiers
- **Raid Objectives**: Assault, Theft, Sabotage, Boss Raid
- **Year 2-3 Schedules**: Iron Marauder escalation → final Marshal campaign

### #30 — Full Machine Catalog [LARGE]
All remaining mechs, vehicles, turrets, traps, workshop upgrades.
- **New Mechs**: Combat Mk II, Heavy Mech, Siege Breaker, Scout Mech
- **Vehicles**: Personal Flyer, Cargo Hauler, Cargo Zeppelin, Battle Zeppelin
- **Workshop Upgrades**: Forge Tier II/III, Fabricator automation, AI Core Tier II/III
- **Additional defense upgrades** as mark variants
