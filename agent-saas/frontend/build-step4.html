Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime? +

Yes! You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle. No lock-in, no contracts.

What are Outcome Credits? +

Outcome Credits are used when your agent completes valuable actions — qualifying a lead, booking an appointment, resolving a support ticket, etc. Each action costs a small number of credits.

How does AI Lead Generation work? +

Your AI agent searches the web daily for companies and contacts that match your target profile. It scores each lead by fit and generates a personalized outreach draft. Available on Growth and above.

What happens when I run out of tokens? +

Your agent will pause until the next billing cycle or you can purchase additional credit packs. We'll notify you when you're running low so you're never caught off guard.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Absolutely. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your agent will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
