🚀 Deploy Your Agent

Get your AI agent in front of customers in minutes. Choose a deployment method below.

💬

Web Chat Widget

Not deployed

Add a chat bubble to any website. Customers click it and talk to your agent instantly. Just paste this code before </body>:

<!-- M.ai.K.R Chat Widget --> <script> window.maikrConfig = { agentId: 'YOUR_AGENT_ID', position: 'right', primaryColor: '#C0A060' }; </script> <script src="https://maikr.pro/js/widget.js"></script>
  • 1Paste the code into your website's HTML before </body>
  • 2Replace YOUR_AGENT_ID with your agent ID (found in Dashboard)
  • 3Save and refresh — the chat bubble appears automatically

Phone & SMS

Not connected

Connect a Twilio phone number so customers can call or text your agent. Works with any US/CA number.

  • 1Sign up at twilio.com (free trial)
  • 2Buy a phone number in Twilio console
  • 3Go to Channels → Twilio and enter your credentials
  • 4Set webhook URL to: https://maikr.pro/api/webhooks/twilio
Open Channels →
✈️

Telegram Bot

Not connected

Deploy your agent as a Telegram bot. Customers message your bot directly — your agent responds.

  • 1Open Telegram, search for @BotFather
  • 2Send /newbot and follow the prompts
  • 3Copy the bot token BotFather gives you
  • 4Go to Channels → Telegram and paste your token
  • 5Click "Setup Webhook" — done! Your bot is live
Open Channels →
