## 📅 30-Day Roadmap

### Week 1: Foundation

| Day | Task | Deliverable |
|-----|------|-------------|

| 2 | Landing page framework | Draft copy, structure |
| 3 | Service packaging | 3 tiers with pricing |
| 4 | Lead capture setup | Email, scheduling, forms |
| 5 | First demo automation | Working proof-of-concept |

### Week 2: Proof

| Day | Task | Deliverable |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 6-7 | Build 3 demo automations | Documented workflows |

### Week 3: Launch

| Day | Task | Deliverable |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 11-12 | Outreach to 20 prospects | Personal emails |
| 13-14 | Post on LinkedIn, Twitter | Launch announcement |
| 15 | List on directories | ClawHub, Upwork, Fiverr |

### Week 4: Iterate

| Day | Task | Deliverable |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 16-18 | Close first clients | 1-3 paying customers |
| 19-21 | Refine based on feedback | Improved offerings |
| 22-24 | Build testimonials | Social proof |
| 25-30 | Scale outreach | 50+ prospects, 5+ clients |
