## 2. Brand Identity

### Tagline
**"The cutting edge of AI"**

### Value Proposition
Small businesses lose 10–20+ hours per week to repetitive tasks that don't need a human — data entry, scheduling, follow-ups, reporting. Avant Garde Institute builds custom AI automation systems that eliminate busywork, reduce errors, and free up your team to focus on what actually grows the business.

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** Small businesses with 5–50 employees
- **Industries:** Professional services, e-commerce, logistics, healthcareadmin, real estate
- **Pain points:** Overwhelmed staff, manual processes slowing growth, inconsistent follow-up, spreadsheet chaos
- **Decision-maker:** Owner or operations manager who's tech-curious but not technical

### Tone of Voice
- **Confident but not arrogant** — "We know AI works, we'll show you"
- **Clear, not jargon-heavy** — Explain benefits, not algorithms
- **Approachable** — Think "helpful engineer" not "sales bro"
- **Professional** — Clean, reliable, trustworthy

## 5. Visual Direction

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Deep Teal | #0D7377 |
| Secondary | Warm Slate | #323232 |
| Accent | Electric Coral | #FF6B6B |
| Background | Off-White / Soft Gray | #F7F9FA |
| Text | Charcoal | #1A1A1A |

*Why: Teal signals tech + trust. Coral adds energy + action. Slate keeps it grounded.*

### Typography
- **Headings:** Sans-serif, bold, clean — e.g., Inter or DM Sans (700 weight)
- **Body:** Highly readable sans-serif — e.g., Inter (400/500 weight)
- Avoid: Serif, overly decorative fonts

### Style
- **Clean & modern** — Generous whitespace, structured grids
- **Not overly "AI"** — Avoid the generic robot/circuit imagery. Use abstract flow diagrams, icons, and real people photos instead
- **Trust signals** — Professional layout, clear CTAs, no clutter
- **Mobile-first** — Must look great on phone

### Imagery Direction
- **Hero Visual:** Scimitar cutting through air — the blade is the "cutting edge" of technology, sleek and powerful
- **Motion:** The scimitar in motion, leaving a trail of light/energy behind the blade
- **Metaphor:** Precision, forward momentum, cutting through complexity/obstacles
- Photos of real small teams working together (not stock "typing on laptop" shots)
- Abstract workflow illustrations (arrows, connected nodes, smooth lines)
- Icons for tools: Make, Zapier, ChatGPT, Notion, Slack, HubSpot
