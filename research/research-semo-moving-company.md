# Moving Company Research: SEMO Region (Southeast Missouri)

## Executive Summary
This report provides market research on starting a moving company in Southeast Missouri (SEMO). The region offers opportunities due to steady population, university presence, manufacturing, and retiree relocation, though competition exists.

---

## 1. Market Analysis

### Key Cities in SEMO
| City | Population (Approx.) | Notes |
|------|---------------------|-------|
| Cape Girardeau | 39,000 | Largest city, regional hub, home to Southeast Missouri State University (~9,000 students) |
| Poplar Bluff | 17,000 | Second largest, manufacturing/retail hub |
| Sikeston | 16,500 | Retail center, close to Mississippi River |
| Kennett | 10,500 | Agricultural region |
| Caruthersville | 6,000 | Northeast Arkansas border area |

### Demand Drivers
- **University moves** - SEMO State students moving to/from campus (fall/spring)
- **Military relocations** - While not a major base in SEMO, nearby regions bring some traffic
- **Retirees** - Southeast Missouri is an affordable retirement area
- **Manufacturing jobs** - Several manufacturing facilities bring temporary/contract workers
- **Agriculture** - Seasonal worker movements
- **Local relocations** - Intra-region moves between SEMO cities

## 2. Competition Landscape

### Existing Moving Companies in SEMO
- **Mayflower Transit** (franchise locations)
- **Allied Van Lines** (brokers)
- **Local independent movers** (typically small operations)
- **Self-storage facilities** that offer moving services

### Competitive Gaps
- Limited premium/white-glove moving services in the region
- Many independent operators lack professional marketing
- Same-day/short-notice availability is often lacking
- Packing services are underrepresented

---

## 3. Regulatory Requirements (Missouri)

### Business Licensing
- **Missouri Secretary of State** - Register as LLC/corporation
- **Local business license** - Check with city/county where headquartered
- **USDOT Number** - Required for interstate transport (if moving across state lines)
- **MC Number** - Motor Carrier authority from FMCSA if operating as carrier

### Insurance Requirements
- **General liability insurance** - Recommended $1-2M minimum
- **Cargo insurance** - Protects customer belongings during transport
- **Workers' compensation** - Required if you have employees
- **Commercial auto insurance** - For trucks/vehicles used in business

### Additional Considerations
- No special state moving license required in Missouri for intrastate moves
- Interstate moves require FMCSA registration
- Consider binding estimates vs. non-binding

## 5. Target Demographics

### Primary Targets (Highest Value)
1. **University students** - Need affordable, flexible moving
   - Book in advance for fall/spring semesters
   - Offer storage options near campus

2. **Military families** - Often require flexible scheduling
   - Government moves through TSP (Transportation Service Provider)
   - Can get on approved mover lists

3. **Seniors/Retirees** - Downsizing moves
   - Need packing/organization services
   - Often prefer local, trusted providers
   - Higher willingness to pay for full service

4. **Manufacturing/corporate relocations** - Contract work potential
   - Build relationships with HR departments
   - Offer corporate accounts with invoicing

### Secondary Targets
- Local moves within SEMO
- College rentals (summer turnover)
- Estate/auction moves

---

## 6. Seasonal Trends

### Peak Season (May - September)
- **Busy:** Memorial Day through Labor Day
- **Highest:** June, July, August
- **Challenge:** Capacity constraints, higher labor costs

### Off-Peak (October - April)
- Slower but steady university-related demand
- November-December can be slow
- January-February is slowest (opportunity for marketing/prep)

### Recommendations
- Build summer crew in May
- Offer off-season discounts to maintain cash flow
- Focus on university moves in spring (May) and late August

## 8. Key Findings & Recommendations

### Strengths of SEMO Market
1. Underserved for professional moving services
2. University creates predictable annual demand
3. Lower operating costs than metro areas
4. Growing retirement community needs moving help

### Challenges
1. Seasonal revenue fluctuation
2. Limited population density = more driving time between jobs
3. Competition from "weekend warriors" and hobby movers
4. Thin margins if not careful with pricing

### Strategic Recommendations
1. **Start in Cape Girardeau** - Maximize population reach
2. **Partner with SEMO University** - Get on housing office referral list
3. **Offer packing services** - Differentiate from basic movers
4. **Build online presence** - Many local competitors have weak web presence
5. **Consider storage integration** - Partner with or offer storage
6. **Focus on seniors** - Less price sensitive, need full service
7. **Get interstate authority** - Opens up routes to Memphis, St. Louis, Nashville
