# Fictional Suppliers for Trap Game

## 1. The Don

- **Real Name:** Antonio "Tony" Salvatori
- **Nickname:** The Don
- **Gang:** The Old Family
- **Personality:** Calculated, old-school honor, speaks softly but commands absolute respect. Never raises his voice, never forgets a slight.
- **Physical Description:** Stocky man in his 60s, silver hair slicked back, immaculate tailored suits, gold pinky ring. Grandfatherly smile that doesn't reach his eyes.
- **Sprite Needs:** Older male, formal attire, distinguished look, gold accessories

---

## 2. Viper

- **Real Name:** Cassandra "Cass" Chen
- **Nickname:** Viper
- **Gang:** The Serpents
- **Personality:** Cold, patient, strikes only when prey is vulnerable. Collects secrets like trophies. Speaks in smooth riddles.
- **Physical Description:** Sleek Asian woman, mid-30s, black hair in sharp angles, tattoos of coiling snakes visible on neck and arms. Always wears green.
- **Sprite Needs:** Female, sleek/cat-like pose, snake tattoos, green color scheme

---

## 3. Rook

- **Real Name:** Marcus "Rook" Williams
- **Nickname:** Rook
- **Gang:** The Crown
- **Personality:** Loyal to a fault, views territory as chessboard. Strategic, protective of crew, views himself as guardian of the block.
- **Physical Description:** Towering black man, bald, heavy build, wears crown-themed jewelry. Scars on knuckles from countless fights.
- **Sprite Needs:** Large male, bald, crown jewelry, regal yet streetwise

---

## 4. Ghost

- **Real Name:** Elena "Ghost" Volkov
- **Nickname:** Ghost
- **Gang:** The Ravens
- **Personality:** Silent, observant, appears from nowhere. Deadpan humor, rarely shows emotion. The one you never see coming.
- **Physical Description:** Pale woman, ghostly white hair, dark circles under eyes, always in black. Moves like smoke.
- **Sprite Needs:** Female, pale/anemic look, dark clothing, ethereal/mysterious vibe

---

## 5. Iron

- **Real Name:** Derek "Iron" O'Brien
- **Nickname:** Iron
- **Gang:** The Iron Hands
- **Personality:** Mechanic at heart, sees people as machines. Fixes problems with tools and torque. Speaks in mechanical metaphors.
- **Physical Description:** Burly white man, mid-40s, beard, perpetual grease stains, arms like tree trunks. Wears leather and steel.
- **Sprite Needs:** Large muscular male, beard, leather biker aesthetic, mechanical vibe

---

## 6. Fang

- **Real Name:** Jerome "Fang" Johnson
- **Nickname:** Fang
- **Gang:** The Jackals
- **Personality:** Opportunistic scavenger, feeds on others' scraps. Fast-talking, slippery, always looking for the main chance.
- **Physical Description:** Skinny African-American man, gold teeth, quick movements, wears scavenged clothing from various crews.
- **Sprite Needs:** Slim male, gold teeth visible, shifty/scavenger aesthetic

---

## 7. Storm

- **Real Name:** Isabella "Storm" Reyes
- **Nickname:** Storm
- **Gang:** The Drift
- **Personality:** Chaotic energy, unpredictable as weather. Hot-headed but fiercely protective of her crew. Plans change every five minutes.
- **Physical Description:** Latina woman, wild curly hair, tattoos covering arms, always moving, nervous energy radiating from her.
- **Sprite Needs:** Female, wild hair, tattooed, energetic/chaotic pose

---

## 8. Shade

- **Real Name:** Kevin "Shade" Park
- **Nickname:** Shade
- **Gang:** The Shadows
- **Personality:** The information broker. Knows everything about everyone. Speaks in whispers, deals in rumors and hard truths.
- **Physical Description:** Androgynous Asian man, youthful looks, hooded eyes, always in shadows. Never seen without his phone.
- **Sprite Needs:** Slim male, mysterious, hooded clothing, tech-savvy look

---

## 9. Blaze

- **Real Name:** Marcus "Blaze" Thompson
- **Nickname:** Blaze
- **Gang:** The Embers
- **Personality:** Hot-tempered, fire-starter. Loves chaos, burns bridges (sometimes literally). Loyal to his crew, hates rivals.
- **Physical Description:** Red-haired white man, burned scar on left side of face, muscular, always wears fire-themed gear.
- **Sprite Needs:** Male with red hair, burn scar, fire-themed clothing, aggressive stance

---

## 10. Frost

- **Real Name:** Yuri "Frost" Petrov
- **Nickname:** Frost
- **Gang:** The Ice
- **Personality:** Icy exterior, never loses composure. Russian background, runs the harder substances. Speaks broken English with heavy accent.
- **Physical Description:** Pale Russian man, buzz cut, ice-blue eyes, intimidating stillness. Always wears dark coats.
- **Sprite Needs:** White male, buzz cut, cold/ice blue eyes, Russian/European look

---

## 11. Razor

- **Real Name:** Tamika "Razor" Davis
- **Nickname:** Razor
- **Gang:** The Blades
- **Personality:** Sharp-tongued, sharper blades. No patience for fools. Runs weapons and protection. Speaks first, thinks never.
- **Physical Description:** Tough black woman, dreads, carries multiple visible blades, scar tissue on hands from fights.
- **Sprite Needs:** Female, dreads, weapon-oriented, tough/scarred look

---

## 12. Byte

- **Real Name:** Alex "Byte" Nguyen
- **Nickname:** Byte
- **Gang:** The Network
- **Personality:** Genius hacker, lives online, socially awkward in person. Deals in digital goods, encryption, surveillance counter-measures.
- **Physical Description:** Young Asian man, skinny, glasses, neon green hair highlights, always has laptop nearby, pale from lack of sunlight.
- **Sprite Needs:** Young male, glasses, cyberpunk/hacker aesthetic, tech-focused
