# Game References

## Top-Down RPGs to Study
- **Undertale** - Character interaction, dialogue systems
- **Stardew Valley** - Pixel art, progression loops
- **Celeste** - Tight controls, feel
- **Lethal Company** - Co-op mission structure (similar to our concept)

## Mission Control Games
- **Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes** - Communication-based missions
- **Space Team** - Co-op spaceship operation

## Station/Space Games
- **FTL: Faster Than Light** - Rogue-like mission structure
- **Space Station 13** - Roleplay, station management
- **Oxygen Not Included** - Base building, systems management

## Pixel Art RPGs
- **Chrono Trigger** - Story integration
- **Mother 3** - Creative UI
- **Hyper Light Drifter** - Visual design masterclass

## Key Takeaways
- **Progression** - Unlocks as reward for completion
- **NPCs as interface** - Our concept aligns with Undertale/Stardew
- **Pixel art standards** - 16x16 to 64x64 characters, tile-based worlds

## What to Steal
1. Mission brief/debrief system (FTL)
2. NPC interaction as gameplay (Undertale)
3. Unlock progression (Stardew)
4. Tight controls (Celeste)
