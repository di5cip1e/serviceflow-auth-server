# Pixel Art Sources

## Free Resources
- **Kenney Assets** - kenney.nl — Best free pixel art, huge collections
- **OpenGameArt** - opengameart.org - Community-driven, mixed quality
- **Itch.io (Free tags)** - itch.io/asset/free — Great finds with search
- **SpriteLand** - spriteland.com - Basic but usable sprites

## Paid Resources
- **Itch.io (Premium)** - Higher quality, support artists
- **GameDev Market** - gamedevmarket.net - Professional assets
- **CraftPix.net** - craftpix.net - Affordable game bundles

## Recommendations
1. **Start with Kenney** - Free, quality, no attribution required
2. **Itch.io Free** - Browse "pixel art" + "top rated"
3. **Hire later** - Custom art when we have budget
