## Contract & SLA Research

### Key Contract Terms
- **Scope:** Define AI systems precisely (models, agents, integrations)
- **IP Rights:** Client owns custom outputs; contractor keeps background IP
- **Disclosure:** List AI systems used, training data sources
- **Bias:** Codify neutrality standards, remediation for non-compliance

### SLA Best Practices
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Uptime | 99.9%+ |
| Response | 4-hour for downtime |
| Bias | <1% flagged outputs |
| Incident | 72-hour reporting |

### For Our Contracts
- Clear scope definition
- IP allocation (client owns custom work)
- Performance guarantees
- Exit strategy
