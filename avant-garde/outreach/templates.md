# Cold Outreach Templates for Automation Consulting

---

## 1. LinkedIn Connection Request

**When to use:** Connect with prospects after engaging with their content or when you've identified them as a good fit.

> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been following your posts on [specific topic - e.g., scaling operations / growing your business] and enjoyed your perspective on [specific insight they shared].
>
> I help [SMB owners / ops managers] like yourself automate repetitive workflows so your team can focus on revenue-driving work instead of manual tasks.
>
> I'd love to connect and share what I'm seeing work well in [their industry/niche].
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Title] | Avant-Garde Automation
> [Optional: 1-sentence credential]

## 2. Upwork Proposal Template

**When to use:** Respond to client postings seeking automation help (Zapier, Make, workflow optimization, etc.)

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> I specialize in automating workflows for [industry/niche] businesses — helping them eliminate repetitive tasks and save 5-15 hours/week.
>
> **What caught my attention:** Your project about [specific task they mentioned]. This is exactly the type of challenge I solve consistently.
>
> **My approach:**
> 1. Map your current workflow to identify bottlenecks
> 2. Build automated solution using [Zapier/Make/custom tools]
> 3. Document everything so your team owns it
>
> **Relevant result:** I helped a [similar client] automate [specific result - e.g., client intake, invoicing, data entry] saving them 10 hours/week.
>
> I'm available to start [date]. Would you be open to a quick call to discuss the details?
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]
> [Portfolio link or brief credential]

## 3. Direct Email to Local Business

**When to use:** Cold outreach to local SMBs (restaurants, salons, retail, HVAC, etc.) via their generic contact form or info@ email.

> Subject: Quick question about [Business Name]'s daily operations
>
> Hi there,
>
> I'm [Your Name] — I work with local [type of business] owners to streamline their operations through automation.
>
> I noticed [specific observation about their business - e.g., you're probably swamped with appointment bookings, or handling orders manually].
>
> Most of my clients save 5-10 hours/week without spending much — usually by automating things like:
>
> - [Example: appointment confirmations and reminders]
> - [Example: collecting payments automatically]
> - [Example: syncing orders to your spreadsheet/CRM]
>
> No tech jargon or big commitments. I'll show you what's possible in a free 15-minute call.
>
> If that's not interesting right now, no worries — just wanted to introduce myself.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Phone number]
> [Website]

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Personalize every message** — reference something specific about them
2. **Keep it short** — 3-4 sentences for connection requests, 5-6 for cold emails
3. **Lead with value** — what's in it for them?
4. **Follow up once** — wait 3-5 days, then a brief nudge
5. **Track your response rate** — tweak what doesn't convert

## 4. Follow-Up Email (3-5 days after initial)

**When to use:** No response from initial outreach after 3-5 days.

> Subject: Just checking in — [their business name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Wanted to bump this to the top of your inbox in case it slipped past.
>
> Quick recap: I help [type of business] owners save 5-10 hours/week through automation — without big software investments or tech complexity.
>
> If it's not a good time, no worries at all. But if you're curious what's possible, I'm happy to share a quick example relevant to [their industry].
>
> Either way, best of luck with [something specific about their business].
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Phone number]

## 5. LinkedIn Follow-Up Post

**When to use:** Engagement-based follow-up after connecting.

>
> Quick question for you: What's the most time-consuming task in your business right now that you wish you could automate?
>
> Drop a comment — I'd love to hear what you're dealing with.
>
> [Your Name] | Automation Consultant

## 6. No-Response Final Touch

**When to use:** After follow-up with no response (last attempt, 7+ days after follow-up).

> Subject: One last thing, then I'll leave you alone 😊
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Totally get it — you're busy. This is my last message on this topic, I promise.
>
> If you ever want to chat about automation, just reply "automation" and I'll follow up. Otherwise, I'll stop reaching out.
>
> Best of luck with [their current goal/project].
>
> — [Your Name]
