## Sending Tips

1. **Personalize** — Add a line about something specific you noticed (recent post, website, reviews)
2. **Send Tuesday-Thursday** — Best open rates
3. **Send morning** (7-9am local time) — Or evenings (7-9pm)
4. **Add to tracking** immediately after sending
