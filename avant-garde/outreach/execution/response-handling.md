# Response Handling Script

> What to do when prospects respond

---

## 🎯 If They Say "Interested"

**Response:** Thank them + schedule discovery call immediately

### Action Steps:
1. **Reply within 1 hour** while interest is hot
2. **Offer 2 specific times** (not "when works for you")
3. **Mention the implementation plan is ready** — creates urgency

### Sample Response (Interested):

> Great! I have two spots available — [Tuesday at 2pm] or [Thursday at 10am] — which works better for a 15-min call?
>
> I've already mapped out what the $497 Starter Package would look like for you, so we'll hit the ground running.

---

## 👋 If They Say "Not Interested" (or "Not Right Now")

**Response:** Thank them + add to nurture list

### Action Steps:
1. **Reply graciously** — don't push
2. **Add to nurture list** in tracking
3. **Optional:** Offer one piece of free value

### Sample Response (Not Interested):

> No worries — totally understand. If things change or you hear from other solopreneurs who could use this, I'd appreciate the referral.
>
> I'll keep you on a lightweight newsletter in case it's helpful down the road.

### Nurture Sequence:
- Month 1: Case study relevant to their industry
- Month 3: Quick tip / blog post
- Month 6: Re-engagement ("Still busy?")

---

## ⏰ If No Response (Follow-Up Sequence)

**Timeline:**
| Touchpoint | When | What |
|------------|------|------|
| 1st follow-up | Day 5 | Original email recap + new angle |
| 2nd follow-up | Day 10 | Social proof / testimonial |
| 3rd follow-up | Day 14 | Final offer / last chance |

### Follow-Up 1 (Day 5):

**Subject:** Quick follow-up — [their business type]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Just circling back on my last email. I know you're busy — wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.
>
> If [pain point] isn't a priority right now, no worries. But if you're curious, I'm happy to share what I've automate for [similar business].
>
> Reply "later" and I'll stop checking in. :)
>
> — Derek

### Follow-Up 2 (Day 10):

**Subject:** [Similar prospect] results + a question

> Hey [Name],
>
> Quick update: I just wrapped up automation for a [photographer/consultant/cleaner] in [city] who was spending 12 hours/week on admin. She's now down to 3.
>
> One question: is the main blocker budget, timing, or just not sure if it's worth it?
>
> Either way — worth a 15-min chat or not, happy to help however I can.
>
> — Derek

### Follow-Up 3 (Day 14 - Final):

**Subject:** Last one — no hard feelings

> Hi [Name],
>
> This is my 3rd attempt — I respect that you might not need this, and that's totally fine.
>
> I'll leave you alone after this. But if you ever want to chat automation, I'm here.
>
> Best of luck with [their business],
> Derek

---

## 🚦 Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | Next Action |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| SENT | Email sent, awaiting response | Wait 5 days |
| REPLIED-INTERESTED | Wants to talk | Schedule call |
| REPLIED-NO | Not interested | Add to nurture |
| FOLLOWING-UP | In follow-up sequence | Continue sequence |
| NURTURE | Moved to long-term list | Quarterly check-ins |
| WON | Became client | Move to clients doc |
