# Prospect Research: SMB Automation Client Acquisition

## Platform Research Summary

### Primary Platforms Where SMBs Look for Automation Help

| Platform | Best For | Avg. Budget | Competition |
|----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Upwork** | Project-based automation work | $500–$2,000 | High |
| **Fiverr** | Quick automation fixes | $100–$500 | Medium-High |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B consulting relationships | $1,500–$5,000+ | Medium |
| **Clutch** | Agency discovery (search-driven) | $2,000–$10,000 | Low |
| **Consultport** | Strategic automation consulting | $5,000+ | Low |

### Key Insights

1. **Upwork** — Strong demand for Zapier/Make automation. SMBs post automation jobs regularly. Budgets align well with our Starter ($497) and Growth ($1,497) packages.

2. **LinkedIn** — Best for relationship-based selling. Case studies and thought leadership work well. Ideal for Scale package ($3,997) and add-ons.

3. **Industry Forums** — Chambers of commerce, industry-specific Slack groups, and local business networking groups are untapped.

4. **Referrals** — Accountants, bookkeepers, and SaaS consultants are natural referrers (they see clients struggling with manual processes daily).

## Rationale

These businesses share common characteristics that make them ideal automation clients:

- **Manual, repetitive tasks** consume significant staff time
- **Growth is constrained** by operational bottlenecks (can't hire fast enough)
- **Technology adoption is low** — they're using spreadsheets, email, and basic tools
- **Budget exists** — they understand ROI from efficiency gains
- **Decision-makers are accessible** — usually owner-operated

**Top 3 Priority Prospects** for cold outreach:
1. **Real Estate Agency** — High lead volume, clear need for fast follow-up, budget-conscious
2. **Digital Marketing Agency** — Tech-savvy but overwhelmed by client management tasks
3. **Home Services Business** — Often run by owners who value time freedom, high referral potential
