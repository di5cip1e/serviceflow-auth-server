## 2. Discovery Call Agenda Template

**Purpose:** 20-30 minute call to qualify the client, understand their needs, and present the solution.

---

### Pre-Call Prep

- [ ] Review client's questionnaire responses
- [ ] Check their website/business (if provided)
- [ ] Pull relevant case study examples
- [ ] Prepare personalized demo (if applicable)

---

### Call Agenda

| Time | Section | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **0-2 min** | **Welcome & Agenda** | "Thanks for your time. Here's what we'll cover..." |
| **2-5 min** | **Understand Their Business** | Ask clarifying questions about their workflow |
| **5-12 min** | **Identify Pain Points** | Deep dive on the #1 task they want to automate |
| **12-18 min** | **Present Solution** | Explain how the automation solves their specific problem |
| **18-22 min** | **Pricing & Next Steps** | Confirm package, explain timeline |
| **22-25 min** | **Questions** | Address any concerns |
| **25-30 min** | **Close** | Confirm they want to proceed, explain next steps |

---

### Qualification Checklist

Confirm the client is a good fit:

- [ ] They have a clear pain point (repetitive task)
- [ ] They use at least 2+ tools that can be integrated
- [ ] They have admin access to their tools
- [ ] Their budget aligns with our packages
- [ ] They have time to participate in 1-2 calls

**If NOT a fit:** Kindly recommend an alternative (Fiverr, internal tool, etc.) and thank them for their time.

---

### Discovery Call Script — Key Questions

1. "Walk me through your typical week. Where does the time go?"
2. "What's the one task you find yourself dreading or doing on autopilot?"
3. "When [X task] happens, what tool are you using? What happens next?"
4. "If we could save you 5 hours this month, what would you do with that time?"
5. "Do you have access to make changes in [tool name]?"

---

### Post-Call Follow-Up

**Within 2 hours:**

- Send thank you email with summary
- Send invoice (if ready to proceed)
- Send onboarding questionnaire

**Template:**
> Subject: Great speaking with you, [Name]!
>
> Thanks for your time today. Here's what we discussed:

>
> Next steps:
> 1. Fill out this quick form: [link]
> 2. I'll send a calendar link for our kickoff call
> 3. Once I receive your questionnaire, I'll begin setup
>
> Questions? Just reply.
