## 1. Response Handling System

### Three Reply Scenarios

| Scenario | Trigger | Response Time | Next Action |
|----------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| **"Yes" / Interested** | "Yes, let's do it," "I'm in," booking link clicked | Within 2 hours | Trigger onboarding |
| **Questions** | "How does this work?" "What about [specific concern]?" | Within 4 hours | Send response framework |
| **"No" / Not Ready** | "Not right now," "Too expensive," no response to follow-up | Within 24 hours | Add to nurture list |

---

### Scenario A: Prospect Says "YES"

**Immediate action (within 2 hours):**

1. **Reply with enthusiasm** — Confirm their interest
2. **Send booking link** — Calendly discovery call or direct to invoice
3. **Send onboarding questionnaire** — Link to Google Form
4. **Create client folder** — `/avant-garde/clients/[name]/`

**Reply Template:**

> Subject: Let's do it — next steps
>
> Awesome, [Name]! Excited to get you set up.
>
> Here's how we start:
> 1. Book 15 minutes here: [Calendly link]
> 2. Fill out this quick form so I can build exactly what you need: [Form link]
> 3. I'll send an invoice for $497 once we're on the call
>
> See you soon!
>
> — Derek

---

### Scenario B: Prospect Asks QUESTIONS

**Response framework (within 4 hours):**

1. **Acknowledge their question** — Show you heard them
2. **Answer directly** — Keep it concise
3. **Add value** — Share a relevant tip or example
4. **Soft close** — Ask if they'd like to proceed

**Common Questions & Ready Answers:**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| "How long does it take?" | "About a week. We do discovery, build, test, and hand off — you approve before I finish." |
| "Do I need tech skills?" | "Zero. I handle everything. You just test it and tell me if it works." |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | "30-day support included. If anything breaks, I fix it — no extra cost." |
| "Can I see examples?" | "Happy to show you during our call — I'll pull up a live demo." |
| "Is $497 one-time?" | "Yes — that's the Starter Package. After 30 days, you own it. Additional changes are $85/hr." |
| "What tools do you use?" | "Zapier or Make — both are no-code. I set it up so it runs on autopilot." |

**Reply Template:**

> Subject: Re: [their subject]
>
> Great question, [Name]!
>
> [Answer here — keep it 2-3 sentences]
>
> [Add value/tip if relevant]
>
> Want to hop on a quick 15-min call? I can show you exactly how it'd work for [their business]: [Book link]
>
> — Derek

---

### Scenario C: Prospect Says "NO" or Goes Cold

**Immediate action (within 24 hours):**

1. **Reply gracefully** — Thank them, leave door open
2. **Add to nurture list** — Track in prospects.md
3. **Schedule follow-up** — 30-day touchpoint

**Reply Template (hard no):**

> Subject: No worries — here's to getting your time back
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Totally understand. When you're ready to reclaim those hours, I'm here.
>
> Quick resource in the meantime: I wrote up [useful tip or link relevant to their pain point] — might help in the meantime.
>
> No pressure. Thanks for your time.
>
> — Derek

**Reply Template (price objection):**

> Subject: Re: Automation pricing
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Totally get it — I want to make this work. Since you're on the fence, here's what I'll do: book the discovery call and if we don't find a fit, I'll send you a detailed DIY guide so you can set it up yourself at no cost.
>
> Fair?
>
> — Derek
