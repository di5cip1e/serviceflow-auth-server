## Tips for Personalization

- **Reference something specific** from their business (recent post, Google review, location, etc.)
- **Keep it short** — 3-4 paragraphs max
- **Match their energy** — casual for local businesses, more professional for larger ops
- **Don't be pushy** — the goal is to stay top-of-mind, not pressure
