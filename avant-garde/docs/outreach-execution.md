## Critical Next Steps

### Before Sending (Immediate)

1. **Get a real booking link** — All emails say "[Book a quick call]" — needs real Calendly/hubspot link
2. **Verify email addresses** — Use Hunter.io to confirm each address is valid
3. **Set up tracking** — Use Mailtrack (Gmail) or HubSpot email tracking

### During Outreach (Days 1-14)

4. **Monitor deliverability** — Check if emails are bouncing
5. **A/B test subject lines** — Try 2-3 variations after first batch
6. **Track in CRM** — Even a simple Google Sheet with columns: Name, Business, Email, Sent Date, Open Date, Reply, Status

### After First Responses (Day 14+)

7. **Qualify quickly** — Ask: "What's your biggest time-waster right now?"
8. **Offer call** — "Happy to show you exactly what I'd build — 15 min, no obligation"
9. **Close** — Offer $497 Starter Package, mention 1-week setup timeline

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Emails go to spam | Use warmup service, personalize more, avoid spam words |
| No responses | A/B test subject lines, try phone follow-ups |
| All replies are "not interested" | Ask "What would help?" — might reveal different need |
| Competitors respond | Have case studies ready, emphasize local Austin focus |
