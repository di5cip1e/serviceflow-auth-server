## 2. Ideal Client Personas

### Persona 1: The Overwhelmed Solopreneur

- **Business Type:** Freelance consultant, coach, or agency of 1
- **Pain Point:** Drowning in administrative tasks — invoicing, lead follow-ups, scheduling
- **Current Reality:** Doing everything manually, working evenings/weekends
- **Budget:** $300–$700 (Starter package)
- **Typical Tools:** Google Sheets, Gmail, maybe QuickBooks
- **Trigger Event:** Missed a big client because follow-up email got buried
- **Ideal Package:** Starter — $497

### Persona 2: The Agency Owner

- **Business Type:** Digital marketing agency, design studio, or consultancy (2–5 people)
- **Pain Point:** Client reporting is manual, social media is inconsistent, lead response is slow
- **Current Reality:** Junior staff spending hours on repetitive tasks
- **Budget:** $1,200–$2,000 (Growth package)
- **Typical Tools:** HubSpot, Buffer, Google Workspace
- **Trigger Event:** Lost a renewal because reporting wasn't "professional enough"
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 3: The E-commerce Store Owner

- **Business Type:** Online store (Shopify, WooCommerce)
- **Pain Point:** Order processing, inventory tracking, customer service follow-ups
- **Current Reality:** Manually updating spreadsheets, chasing suppliers
- **Budget:** $1,000–$2,500
- **Typical Tools:** Shopify, Excel, email
- **Trigger Event:** Double-shipped an order because of poor tracking
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 4: The Professional Services Firm

- **Business Type:** Law firm, accounting practice, real estate group (5–15 people)
- **Pain Point:** Client onboarding is paperwork-heavy, scheduling is a mess, billing is slow
- **Current Reality:** Paralegals/admin staff overwhelmed, partners frustrated
- **Budget:** $2,500–$5,000 (Growth + Scale)
- **Typical Tools:** Clio, QuickBooks, Google Calendar
- **Trigger Event:** Client complained about "taking forever to get started"
- **Ideal Package:** Scale — $3,997

### Persona 5: The Recruiter / Staffing Agency

- **Business Type:** Small recruiting firm (3–8 recruiters)
- **Pain Point:** Candidate communication, interview scheduling, CRM data entry
- **Current Reality:** Recruiters spending 30% of time on data entry
- **Budget:** $1,500–$3,000
- **Typical Tools:** Bullhorn (if enterprise), spreadsheets, Gmail
- **Trigger Event:** Top candidate accepted elsewhere because follow-up was delayed
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 6: The SaaS Founder

- **Business Type:** Early-stage SaaS (2–5 people)
- **Pain Point:** Customer onboarding emails, support ticket routing, billing alerts
- **Current Reality:** Coding features instead of automating operations
- **Budget:** $1,000–$3,000
- **Typical Tools:** Stripe, Intercom, GitHub
- **Trigger Event:** Spent 3 hours manually processing refunds
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 7: The Restaurant/Hospitality Owner

- **Business Type:** Small restaurant chain or hospitality business (10–30 employees)
- **Pain Point:** Staff scheduling, inventory ordering, customer feedback follow-up
- **Current Reality:** Manager doing everything on paper/Excel
- **Budget:** $1,500–$4,000
- **Typical Tools:** Toast, Square, pen and paper
- **Trigger Event:** Food waste due to poor inventory forecasting
- **Ideal Package:** Scale — $3,997

### Persona 8: The Fitness Studio Owner

- **Business Type:** Yoga studio, gym, or personal training business (2–10 staff)
- **Pain Point:** Class scheduling, member retention, automated marketing
- **Current Reality:** Owner manually texting clients for every class
- **Budget:** $700–$2,000
- **Typical Tools:** Mindbody, Mailchimp, spreadsheets
- **Trigger Event:** Member canceled because they "didn't know about the new class schedule"
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 9: The Real Estate Investor/Agent

- **Business Type:** Real estate agent or small property management (1–5 people)
- **Pain Point:** Lead follow-up, showing scheduling, tenant maintenance requests
- **Current Reality:** Missing leads, lost deals, overwhelmed by maintenance calls
- **Typical Tools:** CRM (if any), phone, text
- **Budget:** $800–$2,500
- **Trigger Event:** A lead they "should have closed" went with a competitor
- **Ideal Package:** Growth — $1,497

### Persona 10: The Non-Profit Director

- **Business Type:** Small non-profit (2–8 staff)
- **Pain Point:** Donor follow-up, volunteer scheduling, event registration
- **Current Reality:** Volunteers doing manual data entry, board frustrated
- **Typical Tools:** Mailchimp, Excel, paper signups
- **Budget:** $500–$1,500 (may qualify for discounts)
- **Trigger Event:** Lost a grant because reporting was disorganized
- **Ideal Package:** Starter + add-ons

## 3. Cold Email Templates

### Template A: The Solopreneur (Starter Package)

**Subject:** Quick question — do you still manually follow up with leads?

Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company Name] helps [industry/niche] — that's solid. Most [industry] owners I talk to are spending 10+ hours a week on tasks that could run on autopilot.

Quick example: a [similar business type] client of ours was losing leads because follow-ups got buried in their inbox. We set up a 3-step automation (form → CRM → personalized email) that cut their response time from 4 hours to 4 seconds.

Worth a 15-minute call to see if you'd get similar value?

Cheers,  
[Derek]  
Avant-Garde Automation

---

### Template B: The Agency/Services Firm (Growth Package)

**Subject:** What if your team spent 20 more hours/month on billable work?

Hi [First Name],

Your team at [Company] is clearly busy — but how much of that busy work is actually billable?

Here's what I'm seeing with [similar firms]: junior staff spending hours on:
- Pulling together client reports
- Manually scheduling social posts
- Chasing invoices that should have been sent

We automate those workflows so your team focuses on deliverable work. One client (5-person agency) saved 22 hours/week — that's like adding an extra team member without the headcount cost.

Interested in a quick audit to see where you'd get the biggest time savings?

— Derek  
Avant-Garde Automation

---

### Template C: The E-commerce/SMB Owner (Growth/Scale)

**Subject:** [Company] + 10 hours/week back?

Hey [First Name],

Random question — what's the most repetitive task your team does every single day? For most [industry] owners I talk to, it's one of these:

- Processing orders/invoices
- Following up with past customers
- Updating inventory spreadsheets

I help small businesses automate those workflows so they stop being the "glorified admin." Our typical client sees ROI within 60 days.

Would it make sense to chat for 10 minutes about what [Company]'s biggest bottleneck is?

— Derek  
Avant-Garde Automation

---

### How to Use These Templates

1. **Personalize each email** — Reference something specific about their business (recent post, company size, industry)
2. **Keep it short** — These are designed to get a response, not close on the first email
3. **Follow up** — 60% of responses come from second or third touchpoint
4. **Match package to persona** — Use Template A for solopreneurs, B for agencies, C for everyone else
