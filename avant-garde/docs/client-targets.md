# Client Targets — Where Solopreneurs & SMBs Gather

> Research-backed platform list for Avant Garde automation consulting outreach

---

## Top Platforms for Target Clients

### 1. Facebook Groups

| Group | Members | Focus | Best For |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| **The Rising Tide Society** | 75K+ | Creative entrepreneurs & freelancers | Collaboration-first, values authenticity |
| **Women Entrepreneurs Success Through Community** | 79K+ | Women business owners | High engagement, supportive vibe |
| **Entrepreneurs Only** | 190K+ | General entrepreneur community | Broad reach, no direct selling allowed |
| **Small Business USA** | 78K+ | US-focused SMBs | Practical advice, operational discussions |
| **Freelancing Females** | 65K+ | Women freelancers escaping 9-5 | Job opportunities, resource sharing |
| **Asian Hustle Network** | 100K+ | Global entrepreneurs/creatives | Diverse, all stages welcome |

**Strategy:** Join first, provide value in comments, no direct promo. Use weekly promotion threads if available.

---

### 2. Reddit Communities

| Subreddit | Subscribers | Focus | Best For |
|-----------|-------------|-------|----------|
| **r/smallbusiness** | 1.2M+ | Running SMBs | High volume, automation threads common |
| **r/Entrepreneur** | 2M+ | Entrepreneurship | Broad reach, solopreneur journeys |
| **r/solopreneur** | 150K+ | Solo founders | Niche, tool-heavy (Airtable, Make) |
| **r/freelance** | 500K+ | Freelancing gigs | Client management, admin tips |
| **r/nocode** | 250K+ | No-code tools | Perfect for automation pitch |
| **r/indiehackers** | 300K+ | Bootstrapped startups | Tech-savvy, automation-savvy |

**Strategy:** Answer questions with expertise. Share case studies. No spam. Build karma first.

---

### 3. LinkedIn

- **Targeted with Sales Navigator:** Filter by title (Owner, Founder, CEO) + company size (1-10)
- **Relevant Hashtags:** #SmallBusiness #Solopreneur #Automation #Zapier #NoCode
- **Key Groups:** Search for "Small Business Owners," "Solopreneur," "Women in Business"
- **Content Strategy:** Post case studies showing time saved, share automation tips

**Best Times to Post:** Tue-Thu, 8-10am and 5-6pm local time

---

### 4. Slack Communities

| Community | Focus | Access |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **No Code Founders** | No-code builders, automation | Invite via nocodefounders.com |
| **Zerocoder** | Zero-code development | Invite via zerocoder.com |
| **NoCode MBA** | No-code education | Invite via nocodemba.com |
| **No-code Alley** | Airtable, Zapier, Slack bots | Invite link on website |

**Strategy:** These are warm audiences — they already value automation. Perfect for consulting.

---

### 5. Indie Hacker / Startup Communities

- **Indie Hackers** (indiehackers.com) — 300K+ bootstrappers
- **Product Hunt** — Launch automations, build credibility
- **Founderpath** — Early-stage startup community
- **WIP** (Work In Progress) — Product builders Slack

---

### 6. Local Business Forums

- **Local Chamber of Commerce** groups (online + in-person)
- **Nextdoor Pro** — Local service businesses
- **Yelp Business Forums** — Local SMB discussions

## Recommended Posting Strategies

| Platform | Best Time | Content Type | CTA Approach |
|----------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Facebook Groups | Tue-Thu, 9-11am | Value posts, polls, questions | Soft CTA in comments |
| Reddit | Anytime (sort by new) | Detailed answers, case studies | Subtle link to site |
| LinkedIn | Tue-Thu, 8-10am | Carousel posts, short videos | Direct link in comments |
| Slack | Weekdays, active hours | Help people, share tips | DM for consultation |

---

## Priority Outreach Order

1. **No-code Slack communities** — Warmest audience, already want automation
2. **r/solopreneur + r/nocode** — Reddit niche, high intent
3. **Facebook groups** — Volume, especially The Rising Tide Society
4. **LinkedIn** — B2B credibility, longer sales cycle
5. **Upwork + Clutch** — Direct project bids (mentioned in service-offering.md)
