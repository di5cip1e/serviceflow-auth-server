### 2. Upwork Strategy

**Cost:** Free to create profile, 20% fee on first $500

**Profile Optimization:**
- Title: "Zapier & Make Automation Expert | Workflow Consultant for SMBs"
- Overview: Focus on time savings (5–10 hrs/week), no-code simplicity, industries served
- Portfolio: Link to workflow templates or case studies

**Gig Strategy:**
1. Create "Starter Package" service at $497
2. Create "Growth Package" service at $1,497
3. Bid on jobs with keywords: "Zapier," "automation," "workflow," "integrations"

**Search Filters:**
- Budget: $250–$2,500
- Category: IT & Networking > Software Development > Automation
- Client location: US (English-speaking, higher budgets)

**Daily Action:** Apply to 3–5 relevant jobs per day

## Cold Email Best Practices

1. **Send between 8–10am or 6–7pm** — higher open rates
2. **Follow up on day 4 and day 7** if no response
3. **Keep subject lines under 50 characters** with a question or number
4. **One clear CTA** — book a call, not "let me know if interested"
5. **Track opens** — use a tracking pixel or ask for read receipt
