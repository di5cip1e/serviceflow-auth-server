# Client Intake Form

> What information to collect from new clients when they sign up for automation services.
> Use this as a guide to create your own intake form (Google Forms, Typeform, or direct email).

---

## Purpose

The intake form serves two purposes:
1. **Qualifies the client** — Ensures they're a good fit for our services
2. **Provides implementation details** — Gives us everything needed to build their automation

---

## Form Structure Overview

| Section | Fields | Time to Complete |
|---------|--------|------------------|
| Business Basics | 4 | 1 min |
| Current Tools | 6-8 | 2 min |
| Pain Points | 3 | 2 min |
| Workflow Details | 6-8 | 3 min |
| Access & Setup | 4 | 1 min |
| **Total** | **~25 questions** | **~9 min** |

### Section C: Pain Points

*Purpose: Identify the #1 problem to solve*

12. **What repetitive task takes you the most time each week?** (long text)
    - Be specific: "Every Monday I manually enter new appointments into my calendar" or "I spend 2 hours sending follow-up emails to prospective clients"

13. **What's the ONE thing you wish you could automate today?** (long text)
    - Example: "I want new website form submissions to automatically create a task in my to-do list"

14. **How many hours per week do you spend on manual, repetitive tasks?** (dropdown)
    - [ ] Less than 1 hour
    - [ ] 1-2 hours
    - [ ] 3-5 hours
    - [ ] 6-10 hours
    - [ ] More than 10 hours

15. **What happens if these tasks don't get done?** (long text, optional)
    - Example: "Clients don't get follow-ups, I miss appointments, invoices are late"

---

### Section D: Workflow Selection

*Purpose: Confirm which automation we'll build*

16. **Which workflow would you like automated?** (dropdown — choose ONE for Starter package)
    - [ ] Client intake & inquiry management
    - [ ] Appointment scheduling & confirmations
    - [ ] Invoice creation & payment follow-ups
    - [ ] Lead capture from website/forms
    - [ ] Client communication templates
    - [ ] Social media content scheduling
    - [ ] Not sure — need recommendations
    - [ ] Other: _______________

17. **Describe your ideal outcome** (long text)
    - Example: "When someone fills out my contact form, I want them to get an auto-reply and have their info saved to a spreadsheet automatically"

18. **Current Manual Process** (long text)
    - Step-by-step: What do you do NOW when this happens?
    - Example:
      1. I get an email from the contact form
      2. I copy their info into a spreadsheet
      3. I send them a templated reply
      4. I add a task to my to-do list to follow up in 3 days

19. **Are there any conditions or special cases?** (long text, optional)
    - Example: "Only send the auto-reply on weekdays" or "Skip the follow-up if they live in [state]"

20. **What should NOT happen?** (long text, optional)
    - Example: "Don't send confirmation if they didn't include an email address"

## Follow-Up Questions (If Needed)

If the form doesn't provide enough detail, follow up with:

1. **Screenshots:** "Can you send a screenshot of what [tool] looks like when you use it?"
2. **Process video:** "Could you record a quick 2-minute Loom of you doing this task manually?"
3. **Access verification:** "Can you confirm you can log into [tool] and create a new zap/automation?"
4. **Budget confirmation:** "Just to confirm — you're looking at the $497 Starter package, correct?"

## Quick-Intake Version (Email Alternative)

If you want to skip the form, use this email template:

> Subject: Quick question to get started
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> To set up your automation, I just need a few quick answers:
>
> 1. What tool do you use for [scheduling/invoicing/contacts]?
> 2. What's the current step-by-step process for [task to automate]?
> 3. What should happen automatically? (理想结果)
> 4. Do you have login access to the tools above?
>
> Just reply with a few sentences and we'll get started.
>
> Thanks!  
> Derek

## Data Storage

- **Where:** Google Sheet (private, password-protected)
- **Retention:** Keep until project complete + 1 year
- **Privacy:** Never share client data with third parties
- **Deletion:** Offer to delete upon client request
