# Response Handling Guide — Avant Garde Institute

**Purpose:** Respond professionally to client replies, maximizing conversion while respecting their time

---

## Scenario 1: "Not Interested" (or silence after Day 14)

### Response Template

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Totally understand — not every solution is the right fit at every time. I appreciate you letting me know.
>
> Best of luck with [Business Name]. If things change down the road, feel free to reach out.
>
> Take care,  
> Derek

### Guidelines

- **Don't argue** — Respect their decision immediately
- **Keep it short** — 2-3 sentences
- **Leave the door open** — One sentence, low pressure
- **Archive the contact** — Mark as "Not Interested" in tracker

---

## Scenario 2: "Tell me more" / "I'm interested"

### Response Template

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Glad you're curious! Here's a quick rundown:
>
> [1-2 sentences on the specific problem you solve for them]
>
> The easiest way to see if it's a fit is a quick 30-minute call. No commitment — we'll just talk about your current setup and see if there's a clear opportunity.
>
> Here's my calendar: [insert Calendly link or say "Just reply with a few times that work for you and we'll lock something in"]
>
> Looking forward to it.
>
> Best,  
> Derek

### Guidelines

- **Respond within 24 hours** — Strike while the iron is hot
- **Be specific** — Reference their business, not generic copy
- **Make it easy** — Provide direct calendar link or clear next step
- **Move to discovery** — This is the goal conversion

---

## Scenario 3: Questions

### Response Template (General Questions)

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Great question! [Answer their question directly and concisely]
>
> Happy to dig deeper if it makes sense. Does that help?
>
> Best,  
> Derek

### Response Template (Pricing Questions)

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thanks for asking — I don't have a one-size-fits-all price because every business is different. What I can say is we work with a range of budgets, and the ROI tends to be [time to break even / expected savings].
>
> The best way to get a clear answer is to hop on a short call — I can usually give you a ballpark after understanding your setup.
>
> Does a 15-minute chat make sense?
>
> Best,  
> Derek

### Response Template (Timeline Questions)

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Typically we can have [specific automation] up and running within [timeframe]. It depends a bit on how complex your current setup is, but we've done this plenty of times for [their industry].
>
> Want me to walk you through what the process looks like?
>
> Best,  
> Derek

### Guidelines

- **Answer directly** — Don't dodge, be helpful
- **Keep it conversational** — Not a sales script
- **Bridge to a call** — After answering, suggest next step
- **If you don't know** — Say so, then find out and follow up

---

## Response Speed Expectations

| Type of Reply | Target Response Time |
|---------------|---------------------|
| "Not Interested" | Within 4 hours |
| "Tell me more" | Within 2 hours |
| Questions | Within 4 hours |

---

## Logging Responses

After each response:

1. Update send-tracker.md with:
   - Date of response
   - Type (Interested / Not Interested / Question)
   - Next action taken

2. If "Interested" → Move contact to Discovery pipeline

3. If "Not Interested" → Archive and mark as closed
